Transmission // 3 September 2026

Hunters Stand Firm

Carbineers and Bounty Hunters are more resilient, player Jedi contracts support coordinated hunting parties, faction forces have been reinforced, and some of the galaxy's most important combat and quality-of-life systems have been repaired.

Profession defenses

Built to survive the hunt

  • Carbineer now gains extensive melee, ranged, posture, and state defenses throughout the profession.
  • Master Carbineers receive 70 melee defense and 70 ranged defense from their complete profession tree.
  • Bounty Hunter now gains stronger melee, ranged, knockdown, posture, stun, dizzy, blind, and intimidation defenses.
  • Master Bounty Hunters also receive 70 melee defense and 70 ranged defense while retaining their strong block, dodge, and counterattack progression.
  • Every new modifier is visible directly in the profession sheet.

Master Bounty Hunter

Hunter's Determination

Master Bounty Hunters now visibly receive Hunter's Determination as an innate ability. Every attack made with a ranged, melee, heavy, special-heavy, or thrown weapon has a 75% chance to bypass a player Jedi's lightsaber block.

The ability and its description now appear in the Master Bounty Hunter profession entry.

Player contracts

The hunt becomes a team effort

  • Bounty Hunter mission terminals now reliably display eligible player bounty and player Jedi contracts.
  • Ordinary player and Jedi contracts have clear, appropriate mission titles and descriptions.
  • Up to three Bounty Hunters may accept contracts on the same player Jedi.
  • Those hunters can group together, pursue the same target, and each complete their own contract while participating nearby.
  • Players capable of wielding a lightsaber remain eligible for Jedi bounty missions.

Planetary warfare

Headquarters ready for battle

  • Planetary Headquarters service terminals now stand outside at ground level
  • Existing headquarters automatically receive the corrected terminal layout
  • Headquarters can be attacked without protected terminals obstructing destruction
  • Battlefield turrets now acquire and fire on valid enemy targets
  • Battlefield turret range increased to 100 meters

Faction reinforcements

Stronger companions in war

  • All player faction pets except the AT-AT received 75% more HAM.
  • These pets now carry Heavy armor and stronger non-vulnerable resistances, including lightsaber protection.
  • Existing faction pets receive the improvements without needing to be replaced.
  • The Nightsister Elder faction pet now matches the formidable toughness and resistance profile of the wild Nightsister Elder.

Weapons and equipment

Battlefield upgrades

  • The Nightsister Energy Lance now uses Heavy armor piercing
  • Existing Energy Lances receive the upgraded armor piercing
  • Fresh Mandalorian rebreathers now provide ten sockets
  • Death Watch Overlord blood completion awards the complete Mandalorian armor set

Quality of life

More time fighting, less time waiting

  • Area looting now reaches defeated enemies within 100 meters.
  • Force Armor and Force Shield effects now last for 24 hours.
  • Rank-five player cities can support up to 100 trainers.
  • Dathomir destroy missions generate farther from mission terminals and have more room to find valid exact placement.
  • A static Boba Fett encounter has joined the Death Watch Bunker hunting hub.

Force Sensitive journey

The Old Man speaks again

Players who completed the Tatooine survival trial can once again continue their conversation with the Old Man instead of receiving an empty response. Affected players can simply speak with him again; no progression reset is required.

Client update required

Fully close and reopen the launcher before entering the galaxy.

The launcher will deliver the updated Carbineer and Bounty Hunter profession sheets. All server-side combat, contract, faction-pet, headquarters, and quality-of-life changes are already active.
