Patch notes · November 21, 2026

Three New Worlds · Kashyyyk, Mustafar and Ord Mantell

Close the game and run Update or Scan in the launcher. At a travel terminal, select a new planet on the galaxy map and buy a ticket to its landing. The base interplanetary fare for each new route is 3,000 credits each way.

Each world has its own galaxy-map and datapad icon, planetary map, weather, return shuttle and cloning facility. Open the points of interest in your datapad and approach the coordinates below to earn the corresponding exploration badge.

Kashyyyk · Kachirho Landing (0, 0)

Wookiee pathfinders share the forest with Shadowlands katarns and deepwood webweavers. Watch for Trandoshan hunting captains.

  • Last Lantern Crossing (0, -3000) awards Beneath the Last Lantern.
  • Bocctyy Watch (2300, 0) awards Watcher of the Wroshyrs.
  • Webweaver Hollow (5500, -5100) awards Into the Webweaver Hollow.

Mustafar · Mensix Landing (-2601, 1635)

Cinderback tulrus, obsidian xandanks and Mensix lava fleas roam the surface near the prospectors.

  • The Mensix Crucible (-2240, 1840) awards The Last Pour.
  • The Smoking Forest (-5464, 4110) awards Cinder Walker.
  • Storm Lord Lookout (193, 4163) awards Eye of the Storm Lord.

Ord Mantell · Worlport Landing (830, -1220)

Rustwater scavenger rats and scrapyard pack hounds range through the salvage fields. Worlport scrap breakers work the yards, and brigands form camps and patrols.

  • The Missing Manifest (1150, 2020) awards Last One on the Manifest.
  • Breaker's Cut (-2780, 1420) awards Breaker of the Line.
  • Rustwater Beacon (3360, -3040) awards Keeper of Rustwater.

Rare boss hunts

Across nine planets, 29 level 550 bosses and 11 level 300 guards have joined the hunt. Signature drops include the Adeen Lightsaber, Mustafarian Lightsaber, Qixoni Crystal, Shiny Pearl, Kyber Crystal and AV-21 Landspeeder Schematic.
