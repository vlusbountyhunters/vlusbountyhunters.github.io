Transmission // 14 August 2026

Galaxy Update

Major loot improvements, expanded storage, stronger lightsabers, and accessible player-city progression are now live on VLU Core3.

Loot and crafting

Advanced components

Advanced weapon and combat-medic components are now dramatically more likely to appear in rare-loot, boss, reward-crate, and specialist enemy pools. Their best possible stat rolls have also increased by approximately 25%.

  • Improved weapon-component damage, speed, wound chance, range, durability, cost reduction, and use-count potential.
  • Improved combat-medic power, potency, duration, area, range, charges, and use-count potential.
  • Geonosis SBD advanced weapon-component rewards remain guaranteed.

Medical supplies

Tribal blood components

  • Janta blood: 60% common-pool selection
  • Dantari blood: 60% common-pool selection
  • Kunga blood: 60% common-pool selection
  • Mokk blood: 60% common and 40% elite selection
  • Maximum medical rolls improved by approximately 25%

Quality of life and combat

More freedom across the galaxy

01

Expanded backpacks

Wearable backpacks now hold up to 500 items and can be equipped alongside Jedi robes.

02

Heavy-piercing sabers

Lightsabers now use heavy armor piercing across one-handed, two-handed, polearm, training, and custom variants.

03

Player cities

Only one citizen is now required at every city rank, allowing a settlement to progress to Metropolis with a single citizen.

Deployment complete

The update is live now.
