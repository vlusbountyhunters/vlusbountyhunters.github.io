Patch notes · October 1, 2026 · Changes deployed September 29–October 1

GCW Campaign, Hoth, Rewards and Repairs

These notes list deployed changes. They replace the story format for current updates; older reports remain in the archive.

GCW purchases and delivery fixes

  • Fixed purchases, permanent upgrade redemption, support claims and guild contributions that were being rejected.
  • Use GCW Currency Requisitions at the Imperial or Rebel recruiter matching your allegiance. Payment uses GCW currency, not credits or faction standing.
  • Purchases require confirmation. Paid items blocked by a full inventory can be recovered from the same menu without paying again.
  • The inventory catalogue now contains 34 registered recipes; faction banners are restricted to their matching faction.
  • Basic requisitions: Binary Liquid—12,000; Command Console—15,000; Tactical Map Table—25,000; Equipment Tool Chest—8,000; faction banner—15,000 GCW currency.
  • Nine medical supplies cost 8,000 each and have 50 uses: Field Trauma, Poison Treatment, Disease Treatment, and Health, Action, Strength, Constitution, Quickness and Stamina Wound Kits.

Veteran armor and field support

  • Added Standard, Elite and Legendary Heavy breastplates in Assault, Defender and Field Medic variants: nine new armor choices.
  • Standard costs 50,000, has 80% base resistance to all damage types and +10 role skill modifiers. Elite costs 100,000, has 85% and +20. Legendary costs 150,000, has 90% and +30.
  • Resistances include stun and lightsabers. Each breastplate has 100 Health, Action and Mind encumbrance. These purchases create new armor; they do not upgrade an existing piece.
  • Assault improves weapon accuracy; Defender improves melee/ranged defense, dodge, block and counterattack; Field Medic improves treatment and treatment speed.
  • Healing Station Controller: 25,000 currency; each use restores up to 3,000 Health, Action and Mind.
  • Resupply Station Controller: 20,000 currency; each use provides a 10-use, 1,000-power first-aid kit.
  • Both stations have 100,000 structure health, 30 shared charges and a 20-minute lifetime. Faction allies can use them once per minute; deployment cooldown is one hour.
  • Stations deploy outdoors, outside cities and buildings, subject to safe-placement checks. Eligible enemies can attack them. Stations expire on restart and consumed controllers are not refunded.

Talus campaign and navigation

  • Added four capturable sites near the Talus battlefield: forward camp (approximately −2450, 3410), communications (−2750, 3760), shield (−2450, 4110) and supply depot (−2100, 3760).
  • Recruiter and campaign-console menus can add or refresh four persistent datapad waypoints at the actual console positions. Unrelated waypoints are preserved.
  • Overt Imperial and Rebel players can enlist at a console. Stay within 25 metres and renew capture orders every minute.
  • Uncontested capture takes 180 seconds solo, decreasing by 30 seconds per additional participating account to a minimum of 60 seconds.
  • Living overt enemies contest capture even if they have not enlisted or are mounted. Covert or dead opponents do not contest. Multiple characters on one account do not accelerate capture.
  • Combat-defense credit is limited to once per objective per ten minutes. Communications ownership reveals active courier coordinates through campaign status.

Couriers, faction contracts and seasonal finale

  • Participating factions can dispatch one armored courier each every 30 minutes. Couriers are level 300 with 500,000 HAM and Heavy armor.
  • Escort for at least 60 seconds and reach the depot, or defeat an enemy courier with qualifying damage/healing participation. Couriers expire after 20 minutes; unfinished couriers are cancelled on restart.
  • Successful deliveries build up to three camp supply charges. An owned camp can spend one charge to recover 5,000 in each HAM pool, once per account per ten minutes.
  • Black Sun members can carry supplies or steal enemy communications intelligence. Death Watch members can hunt an enemy courier or capture an enemy shield.
  • Contracts expire after 20 minutes and have a one-hour acceptance cooldown. Independent membership does not permit attacking your own GCW faction.
  • A sufficiently trailing faction receives a 50% bonus for ambush, combat-defense and sabotage awards. The leading faction does not lose existing currency.
  • Campaign seasons last 28 days. During the final two hours, holding communications, shield and depot unlocks an opposing level-350 siege marshal with 3,000,000 HAM, Heavy armor and 18,000–28,000 base damage.
  • Only one successful finale occurs per season. Qualifying damage dealers and healers receive finale participation credit, unlocking the War Table requirement.
  • Each awarded campaign point also gives 50 GCW currency. Campaign-event awards are capped at 2,000 points per account per day. Switching sides imposes a 24-hour campaign reward lock; initial enlistment does not.
  • Seasonal standings reset, but career points, earned items, purchases, guild projects and permanent upgrades remain.

Hoth

  • Restored the outpost, native travel services and four ice caves.
  • Added five points of interest with discoverable exploration badges visible in the character sheet.
  • Added Hoth's custom galaxy-map and datapad icon, planetary map and planet label.
  • Added level-350 Wampa, Tauntaun and Smuggler wilderness variants in a dedicated 12-pack encounter area. Existing Hutt and rare encounters remain.
  • Preserved existing building restrictions and other planets. This update does not add a complete Echo Base dungeon.

Faction rewards, armor paint and houses

  • Added ten Black Sun Bounty Hunter armor pieces to the existing market: Heavy armor with 80% resistances.
  • Added four Death Watch member-communicator paint presets matching Bloodguard, Wraith, Ghost and Overlord NPC schemes for supported Mandalorian armor.
  • Added four Black Sun paint presets matching Thug, Henchman, Guard and Assassin schemes for supported Black Sun Bounty Hunter armor. No unsupported belt paint is included.
  • Painting requires supported, unequipped pieces and preserves their identity, statistics and condition.
  • Repaired YT-1300 and Nightsister-style hut deed placement registration and supplied missing hut assets. Existing deeds, lot costs and normal placement restrictions remain.
  • Repaired ten Bounty Hunter schematic names, including affected existing quality-only names.

Mission, loot, weapon and walker repairs

  • Hollow Signal waypoints persist across relogs and distinguish active from recovering sites. Silent Caravan guards use a wider validated search area.
  • New general and faction outdoor destroy/recon missions use farther placement rules with a 750-metre minimum. Previously issued nearby missions remain valid; in-town delivery, crafting and performance destinations are unchanged.
  • Repaired general destroy-mission groups on Nal Hutta, Mandalore and Taanab; audited Chandrila's groups. High-level Krayt contracts remain ordinary lair missions.
  • Enabled five previously missing clothing-attachment modifiers in existing loot: Force Armor, Light Force Control, Dark Force Control, Light Force Manipulation and Dark Force Manipulation.
  • Repaired the WESTAR's missing projectile asset.
  • Updated walker gait/audio assets. All 13 rideable walker identities now move at 2 metres per second; other speeder speeds are unchanged. Existing cannon and PvP behavior is preserved.

Updating and verification

Close the game and run the current launcher's Scan/Update if you have not downloaded the Hoth, housing, naming and weapon assets. The October 1 campaign update itself requires no additional client download or new launcher installation.

Deployment, startup and automated checks passed. Two-player campaign navigation, capture contention, courier pathing and reward interactions still need in-game confirmation. Please report remaining problems; deployment checks are not a claim that every player interaction has been visually tested.
