Installation sequence

  1. Register: create your secure game account.
  2. Download: wait for the Core3 ZIP to finish completely.
  3. Extract: right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All. Do not run from inside the ZIP.
  4. Install: install the Talon launcher and select your extracted Core3 folder.
  5. Scan: allow the launcher to verify and update all files.
  6. Play: select the Core3 galaxy and enter your account details.
