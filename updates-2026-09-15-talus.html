Frontline transmission // 15 September 2026

The Line on Talus

The first report was dismissed as distant thunder. Then the western horizon flashed red, the ground began to shake, and two armies marched into the old battlefield at once.

The quiet point on the map is quiet no longer.

Talus is at war.

Chapter I // The armies arrive

A Battle That Fights Back

Imperial vanguards have dug into the southern line. Rebel soldiers have answered from the north. Neither side is waiting for a traveler to fire the opening shot: the two forces meet in a live, sustained clash the moment the battlefield awakens.

Every advance is contested. Fallen front-line troops are replaced, elite reinforcements return to the fight, and commanders rally new assaults from behind their lines.

Order of battle

Two Hardened Forces

  • Level 220 Rebel and Imperial vanguards
  • Level 260 commandos and shock troops
  • Level 300 field commanders
  • Heavy armor and strong battlefield resistances
  • Up to 420,000 HAM on commanding officers

Chapter II // No easy flank

Choose Your Banner

The battle has been built as a true two-sided challenge. Rebel and Imperial lines carry comparable strength, while each faction recognizes friend and enemy according to the banner a combatant serves.

A lone warrior may survive the edges of the fight. Breaking the center and reaching an enemy commander is a task for an organized hunting party.

The rhythm of war

Bounded Reinforcements

  • Front-line troops return after five minutes
  • Elite troops return after ten minutes
  • Field commanders return after thirty minutes
  • The encounter remains confined to the established Talus battle point

Chapter III // Spoils from the line

Dispatches and Rare Materiel

Those who survive the crossfire may recover battlefield supplies, advanced components, armor and weapon finds, and intelligence carried out of the fighting in a Talus Frontline Dispatch.

Defeating a field commander opens the rarest cache: a chance at exceptional weapon schematics taken from the command line itself.

Frontline recoveries

Unique Battle Loot

  • Talus Frontline Dispatch
  • Advanced weapon components and rare materials
  • Armor and weapon rewards
  • Commander-exclusive rare schematic pool

The thunder is artillery. The lights are blaster fire.

Report to the Imperial-versus-Rebel battle point on Talus—and decide which line breaks first.
