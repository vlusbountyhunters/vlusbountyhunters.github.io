Transmission // 6 September 2026

The King Beneath Rori

For a moment, the galaxy seemed to breathe easier. The hyperspace lanes opened, cantinas filled their tables, and travelers found new reasons to wander. Then the first reports arrived from beneath Rori: something ancient, furious, and impossibly strong had awakened in the dark.

Chapter I // The roads between stars

Every destination is a doorway

Pilots no longer treat the galaxy's smaller ports as one-way stops. Every registered shuttle and spaceport—including player-city travel points—can now accept interplanetary arrivals through the normal ticketing network.

Journeys that once demanded a return to the great hubs can now continue naturally from world to world. The galaxy is still vast, but its roads finally feel connected.

Cargo from the frontier

More useful discoveries

Armor and clothing attachment loot can now draw from 22 additional functional skill modifiers. The ineffective slope-movement roll has been removed, leaving explorers with a broader and more valuable field of possible finds.

Chapter II // War in plain sight

The invasion leaves the shadows

Black Sun and Death Watch forces have pushed into visible travel hubs across the Outer Rim. Their 162 invasion positions now stand where travelers can encounter them, turning once-familiar arrivals into dangerous front lines.

The galaxy's faction bases have answered in kind. Defensive turrets now strike with four times their former damage, making an assault on a fortified position a decision no commander should take lightly.

Field provisions

A feast before the fight

Every conventional food and drink benefit is now four times as strong as before. Spice remains deliberately untouched; its potency was already where it belonged.

Chapter III // The pilgrimage

Behold the Holy Shrub

High above the sands of Tatooine floats a shrub of such mysterious authority that cartographers have surrendered and marked it as a point of interest. Those who approach it receive a lasting exploration badge and the solemn recognition: “Has glimpsed at the sheer might of The Holy Shrub!”

The Squill Cave and Sennex Cave have also joined Tatooine's planetary map, giving explorers two more reasons to leave the safety of the starports and follow the desert horizon.

Explorer's ledger

Three new marks on Tatooine

  • The Holy Shrub
  • Squill Cave
  • Sennex Cave
  • A persistent badge for reaching the Holy Shrub

Chapter IV // The cave beneath Rori

The Meatlumps have a king

Somewhere in Rori's wilderness, a hidden cave descends into a kingdom of rust, stone, and raw Force power. Meatlump Initiates and Outcasts haunt its passages with strength comparable to the Nightsisters. Deeper within, four Meatlump Protectors guard the final approach.

At the heart of the cave waits the Meatlump King: the galaxy's first world boss. He carries 25 million HAM, Heavy armor, and complete resistance to every damage category except electricity. He and his followers wield Force abilities, and the King returns two hours after his defeat.

World boss doctrine

Bring a hunting party

  • 25 million HAM
  • Heavy armor
  • Electricity is the sole weakness
  • Four elite Force-wielding Protectors
  • Two-hour King respawn

Chapter V // Treasures of the throne

Victory leaves no hunter empty-handed

The Meatlump King guarantees both of his signature treasures. The Meatlump Vibro Motor is an immensely powerful Nightsister-style component, while the Meatlump Energy Wood Staff delivers 9,000–21,000 Energy damage with Heavy armor piercing.

Every Meatlump Protector guarantees the Exceptional Hammer of Carnage, a Kinetic, Heavy-piercing two-handed weapon dealing 8,000–19,000 damage. These are prizes built for the warriors capable of surviving the cave.

Guaranteed spoils

Claim the legend

  • Meatlump Vibro Motor from the King
  • Meatlump Energy Wood Staff from the King
  • Exceptional Hammer of Carnage from every Protector
