Transmission // 24 August 2026

Battlefield Update

Choose your prey, fight closer to home, confront opposing Jedi, and requisition powerful new faction companions.

Mission targeting

Choose the enemy

  • Mission terminals now let players select a preferred destroy-mission target.
  • Available targets display their combat level and are sorted from highest to lowest.
  • The selected preference persists separately for regular, Imperial, and Rebel terminals.
  • Any Target remains available for the traditional mixed selection.
  • Mission pages honor the selected target instead of mixing unrelated enemies into the results.

Local operations

Less travel, more action

  • Destroy missions begin approximately 500–600 meters from the player
  • Terrain and collision safety can place the final battlefield approximately 350–750 meters away
  • Delivery and crafting endpoints remain locally bounded
  • Recon objectives use a nearby 250–500 meter operating area
  • Nearby entertainer targets are selected fairly from eligible performers

Galactic Civil War

Jedi on the battlefield

  • Imperial terminals offer missions against Light Jedi Knights and Masters.
  • Rebel terminals offer missions against Dark Jedi Knights and Masters.
  • Mission eligibility follows the player's current faction and status.
  • Jedi Knights carry correctly aligned lightsaber colors: green or blue for Light Jedi and red for Dark Jedi.
  • Jedi mission lairs use proper NPC objectives and battlefield structures.

Faction reinforcements

Premium companions

  • Acklay faction pet: 750,000 faction points
  • Nightsister Elder faction pet: 1,000,000 faction points
  • Imperial AT-AT faction pet: exactly 4,000,000 faction points
  • Dedicated pet templates keep companions separate from enemy NPC scaling
  • Normal datapad and active-pet limits remain enforced

Mission reliability

Built for repeat operations

  • Target preferences stay with the relevant regular, Imperial, or Rebel terminal.
  • Outdated or ineligible choices fall back safely instead of producing the wrong faction mission.
  • Mission directions now point correctly across every world axis.
  • NPC mission objectives use appropriate battlefield generator structures where needed.

Lightsaber components

Perfect recalculation

  • Crystal and pearl recalculation now selects every configured best-stat endpoint
  • Flawless-quality results are applied consistently
  • No launcher or client download is required for this server-side update

Galaxy online

The Battlefield Update is live.

Visit a mission terminal to select a target and begin your next operation.
