Patch notes · November 21, 2026

Five Public Battlefields and Planetary Waypoints

Close the game, run Update or Scan in the launcher, then reopen the game.

Where to join

Open the Galactic Activity Journal, choose a battle in the activity directory, then select Mark available destination. The waypoint leads to a field post with orders, reward progress and collection for reserved items. Level 350 forces fight on each front before a level 550 boss arrives.

  • Siege of the Keren Causeway · Naboo (1541, 2726). Escort the repair crew to the ion cannon. Capture the droid relay to see incoming barrage coordinates. Interrupt the Viceroy’s Fist during a barrage to expose its shield. Earn a Siege Capacitor for weapon crafting and a chance at the Causeway Siege Projector.
  • The Broken Arrow Convoy · Corellia (−3540, 2910). Stay near the CorSec transports through two ambushes. The medical transport unlocks field treatment if it survives. The munitions transport supplies anti-armor charges. Lure the Highwayman onto the rails and operate the crossing signal. Earn a Salvaged Walker Targeting Assembly and a chance at the Highwayman’s Patrol Carbine.
  • The Last Launch · Hoth (880, −1940). Rebels restore power and escort crews to launch two transports. Imperials capture the targeting station and mark two transports. Rebels who win the race face Frosthammer for an Ablative Plate and a chance at the Scorched Pilot’s Helmet. Imperials face Commander Vale, the Last Guard and a gunship for Last Guard Flight Plating and a chance at the Walker Commander’s Chestplate.
  • The Red Canopy Hunt · Endor (−4790, −2510). Evacuate villagers and escort trap crews to arm snares. Scouts and Rangers can read tracks to reveal Old Redjaw early. Draw his charge into an armed snare. Earn a Redjaw Adrenal Gland for Bio-Engineering and a chance at a Red Canopy Boar-Wolf Companion.
  • Mutiny at the Ash Foundry · Lok (655, 4475). Free the crane operators, seize the furnace and defeat the enforcers. Lure Foreman Krail underneath a suspended load, then signal the operator to strip armor from his exosuit. Earn a Powered Striking Head and a chance at the Foundry Breaker two-handed hammer.

Battle rewards

Each credited victory awards a personal crafting material. The signature item has a 15% chance on ordinary wins and is guaranteed on every fifth credited win for that reward. Damage, pet attacks, healing a participant, escorting crews and completing objectives can earn credit. On Hoth, rewards go to participants on the winning side. If your inventory fills, collect your reserved items at the field post. The front reopens 20 minutes after victory.

Planetary waypoints

The client now recognizes the planet IDs stored in waypoints for Mandalore, Chandrila, Hoth, Taanab and Nal Hutta. Existing waypoints saved with the correct planet ID use the same fix as newly created waypoints.
