Field transmission // 24 September 2026

A Price on Every Shadow

The hunter read the name twice. No Imperial seal. No Rebel authorization. Just a private citizen's credits and a grudge that had survived the cloning chamber. Across the galaxy, the bounty boards were waking up.

A contract of your own

Credits can carry a warning

You no longer need to wait for a personal defeat before putting a price on another player's head. Any profession can fund a contract; the hunters take it from there.

  • Use /placebounty FirstName 100000 to contribute 100,000 credits toward a named player's bounty. Use the character's first name.
  • The default and minimum contribution are 50,000 credits. Enter whole numbers without commas.
  • Contributions add to the target's reward, up to a total of 950,000 credits—below the million-credit VIP contracts.
  • Payment uses bank credits first, then cash. Insufficient funds or a contribution that would exceed the reward ceiling are rejected without charging.
  • You cannot fund a bounty on yourself or another character on your account.
  • An offline player can receive a contribution; their funded bounty becomes available to hunters while they are online.

The cloning chamber remembers

A second chance at revenge

Defeat need not be the last word. The post-PvP revenge offer has been repaired so a fleeing killer cannot simply outrun the decision.

  • After an eligible PvP death, accept the revenge offer to place a bounty on your killer for 50,000 credits.
  • The offer no longer closes just because the killer moves away, and an existing offer can still resolve if the killer logs out.
  • Duel exclusions remain. Cancelling the offer does not charge you, and capped revenge offers do not take another fee.
  • Funded bounties survive relogging and profession surrender. Later contributions are included when a hunter collects an already accepted contract.

A spark beneath the heels

Propelling Boots engage

Cornered in a narrow passage, the hunter stumbled through smoke and poison. Then the boots fired. A moment later, the passage was empty.

  • Master Bounty Hunters can activate /propellingBoots, including existing Masters.
  • Clears stun, blindness, dizziness, intimidation, immobilization and freezing, along with poison, bleeding, disease and fire effects, and returns the hunter to an upright posture.
  • Doubles movement speed for 20 seconds, with a 60-second recharge.
  • Cannot be used while dead, incapacitated or mounted. It does not heal wounds or provide immunity to new effects.

Propelling Boots and the new bounty command have now been confirmed working in-game after the required client update.

Know your profession

The hunter's manual, corrected

The profession sheet now tells a clearer story of the abilities you actually possess.

  • All Bounty Hunter profession-box descriptions have been updated to reflect their actual grants and modifiers.
  • Command descriptions explain damage, states, durations and applicable limitations instead of promising effects the ability does not deliver.
  • Master Bounty Hunter describes Propelling Boots and Hunter's Determination.
  • Hunter's Determination is described accurately as an automatic 50% saber-block bypass chance in the applicable player-versus-player weapon-hit check—not a guaranteed hit.
  • This description pass clarifies existing combat values; it is not another blanket damage increase.

Names written in the mire

Nal Hutta remembers its explorers

Beyond Bilbousa's landing lights, travellers are leaving footprints where the swamp would rather keep its secrets. Their discoveries now leave a lasting mark.

  • Three exploration badges: Explorer of Bilbousa, Explorer of the Abandoned Spice Mine, and Explorer of the Eastern Grime.
  • Earned badges appear in your character sheet and when another player examines your badges.
  • Visit each discovery area to earn its badge. They are not automatically awarded or shown as earned before you explore, and earlier visits require a return.
  • These are exploration achievements, not selectable profession titles.

The quartermaster's ledger

Treasures regain their names

The quartermaster finally put down the crate marked only “Legendary.” Beneath the dust was an object with a name, a purpose, and a place in someone's home.

  • Catalogue label updates cover 568 decoration templates and 546 schematic templates, adding missing names while preserving names that already worked.
  • A follow-up repair addresses affected existing items displaying only “(Exceptional)” or “(Legendary)” instead of their proper identity.
  • Meaningful player-customized names and item bonuses are preserved.
  • These are naming repairs, not changes to item stats, crafting outputs, appearances or loot availability.

Before you leave the hangar

Your launcher needs the new charts

The server update alone cannot teach an old client the new commands. Close the game, update the launcher if needed, select Official Vlu Live, and run Scan/Update against the installation you actually play.

Players on the original launcher who receive a missing Vlu.ClientUpdates.dll error need the first-stage launcher upgrade, followed by the current launcher updater. Close the launcher before each step and select its existing launcher folder, not the game folder. If either upgrade reports an error, stop and contact support; do not uninstall your game or download individual DLLs elsewhere.

Supported newer launchers can use the current updater directly. The latest launcher uses the current patch service. After Scan/Update, vlu_player_bounties_skills_01.tre should be present in your game installation. Launch the game again to load the new commands.
