Transmission // 9 September 2026

The Quartermaster's Warning

The first crates arrived under armed escort: armor recovered from distant campaigns, patterns unseen in the galaxy for years, and Crusader plate built to withstand the fiercest battlefields. Yet beneath the quartermaster's lamps, the celebration gave way to a troubling discovery.

Chapter I // The new arsenal

Old designs return to service

Across Rebel and Imperial supply lines, the newly recovered faction armor catalogue has received its proper battlefield classification. Assault Trooper, Marine, Rebel Assault, Rebel Battle, Scout Trooper, and Stormtrooper variants now stand as Medium armor.

The order reaches beyond fresh requisitions. Armor already carried, stored, or worn by veterans is brought into line as it returns to service, while newly produced pieces inherit the same protection.

Quartermaster's ledger

Faction protection

  • All imported faction armor pieces: Medium
  • Standard, camouflage, and GCW variants included
  • Previously acquired pieces included
  • Future crafted pieces retain the classification

Chapter II // The Crusader's weight

Plate meant for the front line

Crusader Mark II and Mark III armor has been confirmed as Heavy armor from helmet to boots. Its classification now remains steadfast whether the plate has rested for years in a vault or has only just left an armorsmith's workbench.

Where faction armor balances protection and movement, Crusader plate carries the unmistakable weight of a warrior prepared to hold the line.

Crusader doctrine

Heavy by design

  • Crusader Mark II: Heavy
  • Crusader Mark III: Heavy
  • Existing armor included
  • Crafted armor remains Heavy

Chapter III // A flaw in the finish

The armor does not yet look as it should

When the quartermasters turned the new suits beneath the light, some imported armor textures appeared damaged, mismatched, or otherwise incorrect. The protection is active, but the visual finish is not yet worthy of the armor beneath it.

This is a known visual issue and remains under investigation. Armor classifications and protection are unaffected; a future transmission will announce the texture repair once the corrected finishes have been verified.

Known visual issue

Work continues

  • Some imported armor textures display incorrectly
  • The problem is visual, not protective
  • No texture repair is being claimed yet
  • A verified visual correction will follow

The armory remains open

The protection is ready. The finish still needs the artisan's hand.

Faction and Crusader armor classifications are live, including previously acquired pieces. The reported texture defects remain a known visual issue under investigation.
