Patch notes · November 5, 2026

Underworld Operations

Black Sun Mansion Operations Room

  • Added an Operations Room for rival pursuits, expedition rewards, earned material balances, stronghold operations and guild campaign assembly.
  • Guild campaigns require two to eight guild members, each enrolling at the outdoor assembly console.
  • Five completed rival pursuits unlock the furnished honors gallery; ten unlock the command office. Black Sun membership and normal mansion entry restrictions still apply.

Underworld Strongholds

  • Black Sun and Death Watch members can establish wilderness strongholds from mansion or War Forge services on all fifteen supported surface planets.
  • Enrollment explicitly enables local PvP within 160 metres. Each side supports eight players; dual members choose one side for the operation.
  • Defeat four level-350 enforcers and their commander, then hold the area uncontested for two minutes. Operations expire after thirty minutes.
  • Enforcers have 1.5 million HAM; commanders have 5 million HAM. Both have Heavy armor and 75% resistances.
  • Outsiders cannot attack or assist enrolled participants inside an active stronghold. Player pets follow their owner's side.
  • Leaving, dying, loading or disconnecting interrupts capture credit. Leaving starts a thirty-second travel cooldown.
  • Eligible winners earn two beskar fragments, two cipher matrices and one command core. Earned claims remain recoverable after a restart.

Mandalorian War Forge

  • Death Watch services now offer three weapons made with earned expedition materials. All have Heavy armor piercing, 100,000 condition and their normal weapon certifications.
  • Beskar Breach Assembly: T-21 energy rifle, 18,000–36,000 damage, speed 1.5. Player weapon hits reduce nonimmune armor resistance by 10% of its value. Costs four beskar, two cipher and one core.
  • Mandalorian Pursuit Assembly: SR Combat energy pistol, 14,000–28,000 damage, speed 1.0. Player weapon hits have a 25% chance to snare for three seconds, with a shared fifteen-second target cooldown. High-level bosses remain immune. Costs three beskar, four cipher and one core.
  • Mandalorian Resolve Assembly: kinetic Vibro-Axe, 18,000–36,000 damage, speed 1.5. Adds 25 defense against ordinary command states while equipped by a player; guaranteed effects and immunities remain unchanged. Costs five beskar, one cipher and two cores.
  • Completed hunts, boarding operations, heists and rival pursuits contribute earned materials. Existing recorded completions count. Failed inventory delivery retains the paid weapon for recovery without a second charge.

Recurring Rivals

  • Rival pursuits are available across fifteen surface planets, with four specialist tactics and stronger escort groups as victories accumulate.
  • Command encounters include two to six escorts; defeating only the commander does not complete the encounter.
  • After one completed pursuit, a reusable Revenant Signal Kit is available for four expedition tokens. Use it outdoors and out of combat to request another pursuit.
  • Ten victories unlock a free, one-time Revenant Command Trophy and access to The Revenant's Last Word: an 18,000–36,000 energy rifle with Heavy armor piercing, speed 1.5 and 100,000 condition. The rifle costs 64 tokens and requires the Rival Commander Dossier and T-21 certification.
