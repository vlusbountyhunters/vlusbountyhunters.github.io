Patch notes · November 14, 2026

Galactic Activity Journal and Five New Dungeons

Deployed November 14, 2026. No additional client download is required for this update.

Get your Galactic Activity Journal

  • Claim a free portable Journal once per character from an Expedition Bureau's reward catalogue. Keep it in your main inventory and choose Galactic Activity Journal.
  • Browse 19 expeditions and private dungeons with their objectives, requirements, time limits, cooldowns, token rewards and your saved progress.
  • Mark an active outdoor objective or a verified starting location. Interior operations use their objective consoles. If your datapad is full, the Journal retains its previous waypoint.
  • Read twelve directory entries covering the Talus GCW campaign, Death Watch, Black Sun, Underworld Strongholds, the Warren, Geonosian Bio-Lab, Kimogila Cave, Exar Kun Temple, Death Watch Bunker, bounty hunting, Smuggler contracts and planetary exploration.
  • Supported faction and campaign entries show personal progress. Other entries explain where to find their existing quest or badge records. Hidden discoveries retain their discovery rules.
  • Existing faction membership, PvP consent, campaign enrollment and quest requirements still apply. Browsing an activity does not enroll you in PvP or bypass its entry requirements.

Five private dungeons

Each dungeon admits one player, uses existing interior architecture and has six objectives, a 45-minute time limit and a one-hour completion cooldown. Enemies are level-350 elites. Open the dungeon in your Journal and choose Begin dungeon while outdoors on its planet; entry selects a safe nearby site.

Rewards and recovery

  • Each dungeon completion unlocks its own named Victory Display, claimable free once from an Expedition Bureau. Expedition tokens also purchase existing expedition equipment.
  • Use the final objective to register completion and receive your reward. Dungeon objective enemies do not award ordinary loot, XP or harvested resources.
  • Keep rescued captives alive and bring all three within twenty metres of the objective before proceeding.
  • Use Bureau recovery after an interruption. Aborting a private dungeon returns its participant safely and retains previously earned rewards.

November 13: NPC and pet balance

  • NPC and pet Health, Action and Mind pools increased by 75%.
  • Creature combat damage, droid detonation damage and pilot-operated walker cannon damage increased by 75%.
  • Mature creature and droid pet minimum HAM pools increased to 262,500. Existing saved pets receive their increase once when loaded; repeatedly calling them does not stack the increase.
  • Player character base stats and vehicle durability remain unchanged.

These updates are deployed and passed server startup and automated checks. Full player playthroughs and visual checks are still pending. Please report the dungeon or activity name, the objective and what happened if you encounter a problem.
