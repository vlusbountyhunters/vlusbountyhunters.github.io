Patch notes · November 21, 2026

Five Planetary Legends

Close the game, run Update or Scan in the launcher, then reopen the game. The update includes the tracks on Hoth, fused sand on Tatooine and the sound of the Bell-Keeper’s bell.

Where to hunt

  • The Glass Krayt of Tatooine — Look near (3540, −5840) for fused sand. When the ridge moves, get within seven meters of the exposed bedrock. If the dragon’s jaws lodge, close in for eight seconds of increased close-range damage. Its Glass Krayt Fang grants Heavy armor piercing to a melee weapon crafted with it. A flawless pearl and a shed scale trophy can also drop.
  • The Last Basilisk of Mandalore — Follow the forge tracks near (−3410, 2880). Each surviving Death Watch smith repairs the awakened droid. The smiths drop armor segments. Destroying the port cannon stops its sweep and destroying the other stops its targeted shot. Every clear awards an Ancient Actuator. Leave both cannons intact for a 35% roll on a usable Basilisk Siege Cannon.
  • The Bell-Keeper of Dathomir — Listen near (4290, −3150). Iron, Silver and Obsidian guardians drop different staff components. After defeating a guardian, silence its bell within twelve seconds of the Keeper’s call to prevent its return. The personal reward is a Resonant Nightsister Crystal. The rare house trophy rings with the sound heard at the canyon.
  • The White Maw of Hoth — Follow tauntaun tracks near (−2900, 3780). Strike the hanging ice when the Maw lowers its shoulder. Its charge brings down the ice and exposes its shoulder for ten seconds. Creature Handlers and Bio-Engineers can approach within eight meters and select Take a living White Maw DNA sample on the boss to obtain a wampa specimen. The clear awards White Maw Sinew for weapon crafting.
  • The Drowned Collector of Nal Hutta — Find the pumping station near (3120, −2780). Drain the intake, sump and vault in order. While the Collector lives, opening the optional vault releases another creature and selects a pistol, heavy weapon or armor roll for the boss’s loot. Every clear awards a Pressure-Sealed Disruptor Component. The Collector can also drop his wearable diving helmet.

Rewards and return visits

Each boss returns two hours after defeat. Participants earn their own signature material. Pet contributions count for the owner, and nearby groupmates share credit. If your inventory is full, make space and return to the entrance to collect your earned material.
