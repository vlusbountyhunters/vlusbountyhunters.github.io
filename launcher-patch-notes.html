Patch notes · October 9, 2026

Ranger Expeditions, Smuggler Overhaul and Squad Leader Bases

Deployed gameplay changes, listed by feature. Close the game, run Scan / Update Game Files in the launcher, then log in again to refresh learned commands and profession defenses.

Ranger: tracking waypoints

  • Select an Area Track result to create an active waypoint at that target's position. Selecting another result replaces your previous tracking marker without replacing an unrelated personal waypoint with the same name.
  • The marker records the position at selection; it does not continuously follow the target. Results expire after one minute. Targets must still be alive, outdoors, visible to tracking, on the same planet and within 2,048 metres.
  • NPC and player tracking retain their existing training requirements. Master Rangers see creature/NPC levels, with level 300 and higher marked as high threats.

Scout and Ranger traps in combat

  • Thrown traps now work against legally attackable players and NPCs as well as wildlife. Pets remain excluded; existing duel, faction and bounty attack permissions still apply.
  • Player-thrown trap direct damage is multiplied by three. This does not triple control duration or increase NPC attackers' trap damage.
  • Master Ranger has an 80% hit roll against eligible targets. Other characters retain the existing trapping-skill versus defense calculation. Existing states and control immunity can still prevent a state effect.
  • PvP control effects last at most eight seconds and share a 20-second target immunity. Trap damage may still land during control immunity.
  • Range, line of sight and attack permission are checked at throw and impact. Throwing a trap reveals Ranger camouflage, even if it misses. Player targets do not award trapping XP.

New Ranger abilities

  • Ranger Ambush — Movement IV: open combat from Ranger camouflage with a melee or ranged attack dealing 3× damage and +40 accuracy. Uses 1.5× attack delay and weapon Health/Action/Mind costs of 1×/2×/1.5×. Reveals camouflage; unavailable once already in combat. 45-second recharge.
  • Hunter's Focus — Master Ranger: +40 accuracy and +25 melee/ranged defense for 20 seconds. Costs 150 Action and 100 Mind. 90-second recharge.
  • Wilderness Recovery — Support IV: while out of combat inside an active Scout/Ranger camp, restore up to 20% of each HAM pool, capped at 10,000 per pool. Personal recovery only; Squad Leader fortifications do not qualify. Three-minute recharge.
  • Expedition Journal — Novice Ranger: opens expeditions, destination waypoints, progress, field-mark purchases and reputation rewards. Use /expeditionJournal to begin.
  • All four commands have distinct monochrome icons and readable names, help and profession descriptions. Existing trained characters receive the appropriate commands on login without retraining.

Starting Ranger expeditions

  1. Train Novice Ranger, open Expedition Journal and choose an available route.
  2. Visit the three marked wilderness checkpoints in order. At each site, on foot and out of combat, choose Survey this checkpoint within 64 metres.
  3. After all three surveys, harvest the required qualifying wild creatures outdoors on that planet. Then return within 64 metres of the final checkpoint and choose Complete expedition.

  • Master Tracker's Krayt Trophy: 100 field marks and 500 reputation required.

  • Leave room in your inventory for reward delivery. Rejected purchases do not spend field marks.

Squad Leader: field bases and squad orders

  • Available modules include Field Medical, Resupply and Repair Stations, Recon Sensor, Barricade Perimeter, Limited Sentry Turret and Command Uplink. Installation and services spend finite base supplies and use the tier's module slots.
  • Repair restores at most 1,000 condition to an equipped, unbroken weapon. Recon reports visible hostile-contact counts. Command Uplink grants +10 accuracy and defense for 60 seconds. The five service modules share a 60-second recipient cooldown.
  • The Limited Sentry Turret has 30 shots, no ammunition refill, a 100-metre range and a three-second firing cycle. Barricades, turrets and uplinks require a combat-capable Fortified Outpost or Command FOB.
  • Neutral bases serve eligible non-overt owners/group members; combat-base services require a matching overt faction. Existing legal placement rules remain, including no player base construction on Hoth or Mandalore.
  • Focus Fire — Offense IV: +25 squad accuracy against one designated legal enemy for 20 seconds, within 64 metres. Costs 100 Action and 100 Mind; 60-second command and recipient cooldowns.
  • Hold This Position — Defense IV: +25 squad defense for 30 seconds while within 12 metres of the marked position. Leaving the area or group disables the benefit. Costs 100 Action and 100 Mind; 60-second command and recipient cooldowns.
  • Break Contact — Movement IV: clears immobilized and frozen movement states from nearby squad players, not damage-over-time effects or other harmful states. Costs 100 Action and 100 Mind; 90-second command and recipient cooldowns.
  • Emergency Resupply — Support IV: consumes one Squad Supply Pack from the leader's main inventory to restore up to 1,000 Action and 1,000 Mind to nearby squad players within 64 metres. 90-second command and recipient cooldowns; unavailable while trading.
  • These squad orders require group leadership and eligible player recipients. Each has a distinct monochrome icon and readable command/profession help.
  • Form Up: clears dizzy and stunned states and grants +25 defense for 20 seconds to eligible squad players within 64 metres; this defense does not stack with Hold This Position. 60-second command and recipient cooldowns.
  • Retreat: 1.822× movement speed and acceleration for 30 seconds, with −25 attack accuracy. Unmounted recipients cannot combine it with conflicting Burst Run or Force Run effects.
  • Rally: +50 melee/ranged defense and +50 equipped-weapon accuracy for 300 seconds. Steady Aim: ranged aiming bonus of five plus the leader's steady-aim modifier for 300 seconds. Their player buffs do not impose an action-blocking state.

Starting Smuggler missions

  1. Train Novice Smuggler and use /underworldNetwork. Select Find contact for a waypoint on Tatooine, Corellia, Yavin IV or Naboo.
  2. Visit the contact and choose Contraband Courier. Accept the confirmation and pay the 100,000-credit bond.
  3. Travel to the interception waypoint, defeat both required enemy waves, then visit the destination contact and select Deliver cargo.
  • Contracts last 30 minutes and require at least three minutes between acceptance and delivery. There are four attempts per character per UTC day and a ten-minute cooldown after completion or failure.
  • The 100,000-credit bond is returned only on success. Death, cancellation or expiry forfeits it. Cargo is recorded for the accepting courier and cannot be traded.
  • Ordinary routes require the courier's own kills or controlled-pet kills. Crew Operation registers two to six nearby group members at acceptance; only the accepting courier receives the payout. An outsider delivering a killing blow fails the contract.
  • Unfinished fights restart from the first wave after logout or a server restart; the original contract deadline continues. Missing enemies do not count as defeated.

Mission enemies

  • Dedicated interceptors, inspectors and rivals are level 350 with Heavy armor, 80% resistance to all nine damage types, 1,200,000 HAM and 7,000–12,000 base damage.
  • Commanders are level 400 with Heavy armor, the same resistances, 3,500,000 HAM and 14,000–24,000 base damage. Actual damage remains subject to combat defenses.
  • Both waves are mandatory. Higher-tier, high-Heat, crew and bonus-dispatch routes increase opposition. Mission enemies do not provide ordinary XP or loot; completion pays the contract reward.

Smuggler player bounties

  • Contested Courier is explicitly opt-in PvP. Its acceptance screen warns that you additionally spend a nonrefundable 250,000 credits to fund a bounty on yourself.
  • The bounty lasts 30 minutes from acceptance, including after delivery, cancellation, travel or logout. Simply training Smuggler or gaining Heat does not activate it.
  • Bounty terminals identify the contract as Player Bounty: Contested Smuggler and explain its player target, reward, time limit and claim rules.
  • The eligible hunter who delivers the killing blow, including through their controlled pet, receives the single 250,000-credit payout. Group membership does not multiply the reward. Same-account hunts are disallowed.
  • Expired or claimed Smuggler contracts no longer grant tracking or attack rights. Existing Jedi and other player bounties remain separate.

New Smuggler abilities and defenses

  • Cheap Shot — Dirty Fighting I: pistol attack with 2.5× damage and +25 accuracy. A damaging hit reduces target accuracy by 35 for eight seconds, at most once per target every 30 seconds. 20-second recharge.
  • Sabotage Weapon — Dirty Fighting II: pistol attack with 2× damage and +20 accuracy. A damaging hit increases the target's attack delay by 30% for eight seconds, at most once per target every 30 seconds. Does not damage equipment. 30-second recharge.
  • Smoke and Mirrors — Dirty Fighting III: non-damaging pistol burst with a 90% base chance to blind legal enemies within 12 metres of the target for 12 seconds. Maximum target range is 24 metres; normal state defenses apply. 60-second recharge.
  • Loaded Dice — Dirty Fighting IV: +40 pistol accuracy and a 20% chance of 50% additional pistol damage for 15 seconds. Costs 150 Action and 100 Mind. 90-second recharge.
  • Slip the Net — Underworld IV: removes immobilization and freezing and adds 50% movement speed for eight seconds. Other harmful states, damage-over-time effects and PvP flags remain. Costs 150 Action and 100 Mind. 120-second recharge.
  • Nothing Personal — Master Smuggler: pistol finisher against targets at or below 35% Health. 6× damage, reduced to 3× against level-300-or-higher NPCs, with +30 accuracy. 45-second recharge.
  • Underworld Network — Novice Smuggler: opens contacts, contracts, navigation, Heat, reputation, rewards and services.
  • All seven commands have distinct monochrome icons and readable names and descriptions. Training requirements, costs, attack delays and effects are documented in the profession skill descriptions.
  • Across full training, added defenses total +65 ranged defense, +40 dodge and +40 each to blind, dizzy, stun and knockdown defense. Existing trained Smugglers receive the appropriate changes on login. Existing Spice benefits are unchanged.

Slicing and underworld services

  • Equipment slicing offers weapon damage, attack speed or HAM-cost efficiency, and armor protection or encumbrance choices. Existing tool, success, failure and single-slice restrictions remain.
  • Shipping terminals offer one slice per contract, consuming an Encrypted Slicing Chip: reroute with Slicing I, bypass customs with Slicing III, or add a bonus dispatch with Slicing IV.
  • Bonus dispatch adds opposition, including another final-wave commander, and awards 25% additional contract credits plus five marks and reputation. Rerouting never resets the deadline or removes bounty exposure.
  • A 200,000-credit customs bribe or an Inspection Pass removes the extra inspector, not the mandatory mission waves.
  • At contacts, laying low costs 100,000 credits and removes 20 Heat when you have no active shipment. Safehouse travel to the next contact costs 25,000 credits and is unavailable with cargo, combat, bounty exposure or other disqualifying PvP/visibility conditions.
  • A Forged Transponder removes 30 Heat outside combat. An Escape Toolkit provides 25% movement speed for five seconds, with a 180-second recharge; it does not remove PvP flags.

Patch notes · October 4, 2026 · Changes deployed October 1–4

Loot Expansion, Factory Repairs and Faction Updates

These notes cover deployed changes since the October 1 report. Older patch notes remain available in the archive.

Loot variety and components

  • Added or repaired 718 loot reward entries spanning weapons, components, crystals, schematics, clothing, jewelry, decorations, vehicles, housing and supply crates. Entries include variants; this is not a count of unique new models.
  • Added 166 weapon loot variants across 153 existing weapon types. Existing weapon statistics are not globally multiplied by this update.
  • Added Death Watch Siege, Black Sun Precision, Aa'kuan Battle, Binayre Quickfire, Hutt Reinforced, Sennex Wounding and Rorgungan War component families. Rifle barrels, power handlers and sword cores use the galaxy's existing advanced-component strength, with family-specific tradeoffs of up to 10% on supported properties.
  • Added named crystal choices, Premium and Flawless Krayt pearl grades, and additional relic components. Component stack ranges remain based on existing local components rather than being replaced wholesale.
  • Eligible level-80-and-higher faction enemies receive additional themed loot opportunities. Dedicated wilderness enemies on Hoth, Chandrila, Mandalore and Nal Hutta, selected named bosses and turf commanders receive additional appropriate rewards.
  • Existing enemy loot slots are preserved. The general rare-reward selection now includes the expanded catalogue without removing its previous reward choices.
  • Service NPCs, pets, invulnerable actors and ordinary low-level enemies are excluded from the new themed rolls. Exclusive Meatlump and Big Tooth boss rewards remain protected from general loot distribution.
  • Duplicate themed weapon components can be sold to an arms junk dealer for 250–500 credits.

Schematics, decorations, vehicles and houses

  • Enabled additional banner, plant, rug, Marauder armor, Jedi wearable, lightsaber and vehicle schematic rewards. Repaired recipe learning, missing ingredients and affected crafting-product links.
  • Gave 65 recovered recipes distinct names and descriptions so they no longer share misleading fallback identities in crafting lists.
  • Corrected ten Bounty Hunter armor blueprint loot rewards to use their existing learnable schematics. Master Armorsmith requirements, one-use learning, recipe costs and existing recruiter prices remain unchanged.
  • Restored vehicle crafting components, Jedi Fabric Treatment and the Ancient Blackwing Crystal Pack using appropriate existing component balance.
  • Completed housing recipes for the AT-AT house, Commando Bunker, small windowed Naboo and Tatooine houses, and Yoda's Hut. Added complete recipes for the Basilisk War Droid, crafted Flare-S and Krayt Skull Backpack using comparable existing recipe costs.
  • The Tantive IV vehicle recipe now uses the AV-21 recipe requirements: 1,125 nonferrous metal, 3,125 ferrous metal and one AV-21 power plant.
  • Expanded vehicle-deed loot includes the Hover Chair, XP-38, Military Transport, Panning Droid, Snowspeeder, RIC-920, Gasgano's and Mawhonic's podracers, HK-47 Jetpack and Republic Gunship.
  • Expanded decorative loot includes paintings, botanical specimens, gardens, holocron display relics and seasonal displays. Decorative holocrons are display items, not Jedi-unlock consumables.
  • Repaired missing supporting assets for the deployed catalogue and adjusted affected house terminal positions to their interior floors. Some recovered items use compatible existing appearances.
  • The RIS diagram now has the readable name RIS Armor Blueprint.
  • Mandalorian armor retains its Death Watch Bunker assembly requirements. Existing lot and placement restrictions remain, including the building restrictions on Hoth and Mandalore.

Supply crates and faction vouchers

  • Enabled Artifact, World Treasure, Event Reward, Rare, Exceptional and Legendary Supply Crates, plus Common, Rare and Event Supply Caches and the Recycled Material Supply Crate.
  • The Recycled Material Supply Crate provides twelve 1,000-unit recycled-resource stacks.
  • Crate opening checks reward availability and inventory capacity before completing delivery. Keep the crate directly in your inventory and finish combat or trading before opening it.
  • Imperial and Rebel vouchers redeem for 1,000 ordinary faction standing—not GCW spending currency. You must have room below the standing cap for the full amount; rejected redemption does not consume the voucher.

Factory and inventory repairs

  • Factories now consume the required component units from crated batches instead of spending a whole component batch for one ingredient.
  • Partly consumed component batches retain their remaining units across saves. Extraction and splitting preserve the remaining total; existing crates are not replenished.
  • Reassigning a manufacturing blueprint no longer duplicates its ingredient-consumption entries.
  • If a factory cannot insert its finished output, it stops before consuming the inputs for that output.
  • Ordinary loose stacks and medicine-charge behavior remain unchanged. Diagnostic circuits retain their existing 50-item electronics factory crates.
  • Player inventories now hold 1,000 items. Existing characters receive the increased capacity on login. Banks and backpacks are unchanged by this inventory update.

GCW camps, capture and deliveries

  • Repaired the Talus Forward Camps: Imperial at approximately (−2840, 3200), Rebel at (−2150, 3290). Each has two tents, a faction banner, a tool chest and an operations console.
  • Campaign navigation provides six waypoints covering the two camps and four capture objectives.
  • Objective menus now offer Begin Capture / Renew Order. Orders last ten minutes, allowing the three-minute solo capture to finish without repeatedly renewing. This supersedes the one-minute renewal guidance in the October 1 notes.
  • Capture requires an overt, living, outdoors, dismounted participant within 25 metres. Enemy opposition interrupts capture. The staging camps themselves are not capture objectives.
  • Repaired delivery of all nine GCW medical kits and resupply first-aid kits. Existing paid deliveries can be recovered through the same requisition menu without paying again.
  • New and pending GCW reward breastplates use Imperial Stormtrooper or Rebel Marine appearances instead of Composite. Legendary Imperial and Marshal Imperial breastplates have pauldrons; Rebel versions do not. Statistics are preserved, and already-delivered armor is not replaced.

Criminal Turf Wars

  • Death Watch and Black Sun members can access Criminal Turf Wars through their recruiters, contacts or field communicators.
  • Three outdoor operations are available: Outer Rim Arms Depot on Rori, Contraband Transit Route on Talus and Underworld Safehouse on Tatooine. Menus supply waypoints to their current locations; refresh them after a restart.
  • Enroll for one membership at the operation console. Up to eight members can gather for three minutes before deployment. Defeat four defenders, then two reinforcements and their commander; clear combat and hold within 30 metres for 60 seconds.
  • Opposition rotates between Aa'kuans, Binayre Pirates, the Hutt Cartel, Sennex and Rorgungans.
  • Enforcers are level 350 with 750,000 HAM, Heavy armor, 75% resistance to all nine damage types and 6,000–9,000 base damage. Commanders have 2,000,000 HAM and 12,000–18,000 base damage with the same level and defenses.
  • Operations expire after 20 minutes. Leaving the area, logging out, dying, mounting or resigning removes participation; an empty crew cancels the operation. Unenrolled interference that delivers a killing blow cancels the operation.
  • Successful eligible participants with at least 30 seconds of attendance receive 250,000 credits, 50 merit and 250 faction standing. Death Watch members also receive two war trophies.
  • Rewards are limited to four paid wins per character per UTC day, with a shared 30-minute reward cooldown across both memberships. Control lasts 30 minutes and does not provide continuous payouts.
  • This is opt-in NPC warfare; it does not add forced PvP hostility between Death Watch and Black Sun players. Unfinished operations and unclaimed temporary completion rewards do not survive a restart.

Faction equipment and communicator menus

  • Black Sun reward armor and Death Watch Veteran armor now have 50,000 maximum condition. Existing pieces update when loaded, preserving their wear percentage and destroyed state; later repair losses still persist.
  • Ordinary crafted Bounty Hunter armor, resistances, slicing and normal condition decay are unchanged by that condition adjustment.
  • Death Watch and Black Sun field communicators now use a single activation entry that opens their action list, avoiding crowded radial menus. Existing contract mechanics and reward amounts are unchanged.
  • Repaired all ten Bounty Hunter armor crafting recipes. Schematics remain learnable by Master Armorsmiths and cost 1,000,000 credits each at the Black Sun recruiter.

Combat, walkers and Force-sensitive progression

  • Player Tumble to Standing now spends all remaining positive Action, whether the pool is full or partly depleted. It cannot be used with no Action. An accepted attempt still spends Action if dizziness causes a fall.
  • Attempts rejected by the existing state, movement or delay restrictions do not spend Action. Tumble to Kneeling, Tumble to Prone and NPC tumble costs are unchanged.
  • All thirteen rideable walker types now have Heavy armor with 75% special protection against all nine damage types.
  • Uncontrolled NPC attacks against players now have minimum hit chances of 85% below level 100, 90% at levels 100–199, 95% at 200–299 and 99% at 300 or higher. Player-controlled pets, PvP and NPC-versus-NPC combat are unchanged by these hit-chance floors.
  • After the existing Force-sensitive unlock, successful real and practice crafting—including tailoring—each award 250 Force-sensitive Crafting XP. Ordinary crafting XP is unchanged.
  • Genuine dancing and music performance ticks now award Force-sensitive Senses XP without spending ordinary XP. Fractional progress carries forward; awards stop once all four Force-sensitive branches are mastered.
  • Real healing can award Force-sensitive XP without the earlier pre-unlock support cooldown blocking it. Self-healing, same-account targets, NPC targets and healing that restores nothing remain excluded.
  • Paemos no longer converts ordinary XP into Force-sensitive XP. Existing experience and trained skills are preserved.

Interior placement and launcher updates

  • Corrected room placement for all 32 Meatlump cave NPCs, both planetary-headquarters recruiters, and the Death Watch quartermaster and two crafting stations. Their combat statistics, loot, respawns and base-destruction rules are unchanged by these placement repairs.
  • Website download links and historical launcher-download links now lead to the current compatible launcher package.
  • Close the game and run the launcher's Scan/Update before playing to download the loot expansion's supporting assets and crafting names. Use the download page if you need the current launcher.

Scope and remaining verification

These changes are deployed and passed server startup and automated checks. The wider catalogue is not complete: unfinished entries remain disabled. Player confirmation of rendering, crafting, crate delivery and multiplayer interactions is still welcome. Please report the exact item or recipe name and what happened if you encounter a problem.
