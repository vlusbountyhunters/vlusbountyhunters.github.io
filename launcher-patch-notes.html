Galactic Landmarks and Exploration Badges

Close the game, run Update or Scan in the launcher, then reopen the game. Open the points of interest in your datapad and travel to the listed coordinates. Walking into each discovery area awards its permanent exploration badge, including when the defenders are respawning.

Ten new landmarks

  • The Last Light Array · Yavin IV (-1480, -1870). Restore power to hear the coded transmission and awaken a security droid. Earn Still Listening. The local leader is the Last Light Interdictor.
  • The Jawa Exchange of Questionable Parts · Tatooine (-3230, -4940). Listen to a protocol droid bid on its own leg. Earn One Previous Owner. The local leader is the Exchange Debt Collector.
  • The Wind Harp of the Grass Sea · Dantooine (-3450, -5310). Dance or play music inside the spires to hear the second harmony. Earn Heard the Grasslands Sing. The local leader is the Grass Sea Broodmother.
  • The Path of Shed Chains · Dathomir (2285, -1430). Follow the discarded restraints from the labor camp and read the escapees’ names. Earn Walked with the Free. The local leader is the Keeper of the Iron Lash.
  • The First Keel · Corellia (1120, 3460). Read the names beneath the unfinished freighter hull. Earn Signed in Durasteel. The local leader is the Keelbreaker Yard Boss.
  • The Lantern Ferry · Rori (-1835, 3410). Ring the near landing’s bell and cross to the answering lantern to earn the badge. Earn Fare Paid in Light. The local leader is the Lantern Ferry Tollmaster.
  • The Kettleworks · Lok (2750, -4180). Watch the red lamp. You have five seconds to clear the nine metres around the wheel before the steam hits. Earn Walked the Boiling Iron. The local leader is the Kettleworks Salvage Foreman.
  • The Skyhook in the Branches · Endor (4320, -2180). Follow suspended crates to the lookout beside the cargo lifter. Earn Above the Wreckage. The local leader is the Skyhook Blood Marauder.
  • The Queen’s Empty Chair · Naboo (-5640, -202). Read the flood memorial beside the stone chair. Earn Witness to the Vigil. The local leader is the Reedbank Matriarch.
  • Dead Reckoning Station · Talus (-3370, 2960). Follow the approach lights to the customs booth and request landing clearance. Earn Cleared to Land. The local leader is the Dead Reckoning Port Captain.

Each site has two level 350 guards with 1.25 million base HAM and a level 400 leader with 3.2 million base HAM. All wear Heavy armor. Guards return after ten minutes and leaders after twenty. At the Jawa Exchange and the Queen’s Empty Chair, defenders retaliate when challenged. At the other eight sites, they attack on sight.

Five earlier frontier discoveries

  • Giants’ Rest · Mandalore (1750, -1167). Look for a field of giant rib cages south of the equator. Walk between the bones. Earn Among Giants.
  • Green Lantern Market · Nal Hutta (-780, 1479). In southern Bilbousa, follow the green streetlamps to the merchant tents. Look for the round brazier beside the banners. Earn Under the Green Lanterns.
  • The Solitary Stone · Taanab (264, -6182). Far west of Starhunter Station, a single monolith rises from the southern plain. Approach its foot. Earn Keeper of the Quiet Mile.
  • Hanna’s Blue Garden · Chandrila (686, -2882). Walk east from central Hanna City to the garden ringed by blue lamps. Follow their light to the middle. Earn Beneath the Blue Lights.
  • Wreckside Bivouac · Hoth (-95, -2047). Southwest of Scavenger Outpost, two tents and a campfire shelter beside a broken freighter. Approach the fire. Earn Shelter in the White.

Each frontier site also has two level 350 defenders and a level 400 leader. The market crew on Nal Hutta and the garden crew on Chandrila fight when challenged.

Living Galaxy: Convoys, Live Bounties and Underworld Operations

Deployed November 16, 2026. Close the game, update or scan through the launcher, then reopen the game.

Where to begin

  • Use your portable Galactic Activity Journal to browse operations, follow objectives and find the new live-bounty and entertainer options. Expedition Bureaus also provide operation choices and a free Journal through their reward catalogue.
  • Moving convoy and Cantina Intelligence encounters can begin outdoors on Tatooine, Corellia, Naboo, Lok, Mandalore or Chandrila, subject to normal wilderness and activity restrictions.

Moving faction convoys

  • Added Faction Convoy: Escort and Faction Convoy: Ambush, featuring Imperial, Rebel, Black Sun or Death Watch cargo couriers.
  • Four couriers physically travel through three checkpoints. Escort them against successive ambushes, or defeat four convoy guards and seize their cargo.
  • Seizing one cargo unit requires eight continuous seconds within eight metres of its courier while out of combat. Destroyed cargo gives no reward.
  • Earn two expedition tokens per delivered or seized unit, up to eight. Return to the starting console to settle after the cargo is resolved and all guards are defeated. Rewards belong to the player who started the operation.
  • Operations have a thirty-minute limit and one-hour cooldown. Blocked routes and abandoned encounters end without granting credit for an arrival that never occurred.

Capture player bounties alive

  • With an active player-bounty mission, select its actual target, incapacitate them in open wilderness outside cities, approach within eight metres and choose Capture selected live bounty target in your Journal.
  • Both characters must be on different accounts. Mounted, hidden, invulnerable, protected or otherwise ineligible targets cannot be captured; normal combat eligibility still applies.
  • A successful capture marks a Live Bounty Pickup. Walk the captive there within three minutes. Both players must be within eight metres of the pickup, and the hunter must be out of combat, to deliver.
  • Captives cannot move independently, attack, use ordinary action commands or travel while restrained. Chat and logout remain available.
  • Escape becomes available after fifteen seconds and takes eight seconds. An escape prompt appears even without a Journal; the Journal also offers escape. Disconnecting, excessive separation, invalid custody or the time limit releases the captive. Release gives five minutes of protection against immediate recapture.
  • Successful live delivery pays the bounty without applying a death penalty to the captive. Other contracts for the same bounty class are retired, preventing another claim against the already-delivered bounty.

Defector rescue and interception

  • Black Sun and Death Watch members can request or mark Defector Rescue and Interception operations through the Journal or an Expedition Bureau.
  • Each fighter must explicitly enroll at the operation console before participating in its local PvP. Marking a waypoint or requesting the operation does not enroll you or change your faction.
  • Defeat the guards and escort the defector toward the appropriate extraction: east for Black Sun, west for Death Watch. The defector follows the uncontested side, stops when opponents contest the escort, and changes direction when control changes.
  • Extraction requires the defector's actual arrival and a two-minute uncontested hold. Each eligible participant on the winning side earns two beskar fragments, two cipher matrices and one command core. Recover earned rewards at the console or through faction operations.

Entertainer rumors and hidden quarry

  • Novice Entertainers or higher can dance or play music inside a supported cantina and choose Gather rumors while performing in a cantina in the Journal.
  • Three uninterrupted minutes of performance earns one lead, at most once per hour. Stopping performance cancels gathering. The earned lead expires after one hour.
  • Follow the lead through Cantina Intelligence: Hidden Quarry, defeat its named commander and two escorts, and recover the cache for twelve expedition tokens and an intelligence collection record.
  • Possible quarry identities include The Silent Broker, Ashen Courier and The Masked Relic Keeper. The encounter has a twenty-minute limit and one-hour cooldown.

More variety in existing adventures

  • The five private dungeons can now add a power failure, an additional captive, two rival raiders or a timed evacuation. Some runs remain unchanged. Power failures add forty-five seconds to the second objective; timed evacuations allow three minutes for the final objective. Completing a run with its added complication awards four bonus expedition tokens.
  • Contested salvage now requires defeating the enemy prototype walker and escorts, then protecting an engineer through three waves of four enemies. Stay within forty metres for ninety uninterrupted seconds and defeat all twelve attackers. Losing the engineer fails the operation; successful salvage retains its six-token reward.
  • Walker Boarding: Breach and Recover adds an enemy NPC walker encounter: disable its leg drive, breach the cockpit for forty-five seconds, defeat four disembarking crew and recover orders. Completion awards ten expedition tokens and a collection record. Twenty-minute limit, one-hour cooldown. This operation does not capture or transfer player-owned walkers.
  • The Mandalorian Great Hunt now varies prey with the day and recent hunting pressure. Inspect feeding and nesting clues; defended nests contain two noncombatant young and can attract up to four additional adults. Successful hunts increase pressure, which recovers over time.
  • Completed raids now influence a recurring named rival, their tactics and escort strength. After two completed raids, rivals can pursue eligible players in the wilderness between other activities. Rival abandonment has a one-hour cooldown.
  • Smuggler customs inspections add bluff, conceal and fight choices. Profession, reputation and Heat affect the outcome. A failed bluff or concealment adds ten Heat and an extra enforcer to each of the two required waves; a successful attempt still requires completing both waves.

Payments, cleanup and recent repairs

  • Inspection bribes now keep payment and inspection progress together. Failed payment attempts leave credits and progress intact.
  • Bounty payments and contract completion are saved together. Completed missions are removed after settlement, and reconnecting or restarting cannot pay a settled contract again.
  • If a death-bounty payment cannot be completed, the contract remains unpaid and a message explains the failure. Contact staff if it does not recover. Live delivery can retry payment while custody remains valid.
  • Fixed the walker laser-cannon toolbar action so it uses the same working firing path as the radial option; its monochrome icon and description are included in the launcher update.
  • Fixed the faction recruiter's quartermaster menu selections, including category navigation and purchase callbacks. Close and reopen any recruiter window that was already open when the repair was applied.
  • Restored missing voice resources used by encounter NPCs. Update through the launcher before playing.

If an encounter gets stuck, report the activity, planet, objective and action attempted. For a bounty issue, include whether you were capturing, escaping or delivering.

Creature Handler Overhaul

Close the game and run Scan/Update in the launcher before reconnecting. Existing trained characters receive their updated profession abilities and modifiers on login.

Commands and profession display

  • Added eight named commands with distinct monochrome icons and profession-sheet descriptions: Protect Me, Sic 'Em, Rally Beast, Break Free, Primal Fury, Emergency Recall, Alpha Bond and Pack Tactics.
  • Protect Me, Sic 'Em and Rally Beast are alternate names for Guardian's Bond, Savage Pursuit and Primal Rally. Each shares the original trained technique requirement and cooldown; they do not provide additional casts.
  • Protect Me: a selected Guardian intercepts 25% of direct Health damage for 12 seconds, capped at 2,500 per hit and 10,000 total. The transfer cannot kill the pet. Recharge: 90 seconds.
  • Sic 'Em: the selected pet gains 50% movement speed and +10 accuracy for eight seconds, plus 3 accuracy per Role Mastery rank. A Stalker's next successful hit also attempts a short snare, camouflage exposure and combat delay. Recharge: 45 seconds.
  • Rally Beast: restores 20% of pet HAM, or 25% during Apex Instinct, plus 1% per bond level beyond one and 2% per Recovery rank. Recovery is capped at 30,000 per pool. Removes one root, snare, dizzy, stunned or blinded state. Support companions also cleanse the handler, nearby owned pets and eligible group members within 16 metres. Recharge and shared group-cleanse cooldown: 90 seconds.
  • Break Free: removes root and snare from the selected creature companion. Recharge: 90 seconds; learned at Creature Training IV.
  • Primal Fury: grants the selected pet 20% more direct damage for 15 seconds, at the cost of taking 15% more direct damage. Recharge: 90 seconds; requires Master Creature Handler.
  • Emergency Recall: directs the selected pet to follow through normal pathfinding and attempt peace. It does not teleport, bypass walls, remove roots or force opponents out of combat. Cannot recall a creature carrying a rider. Recharge: 30 seconds; learned at Novice.
  • Companion abilities require a living owned creature within 48 metres and line of sight. Normal legal attack and assistance restrictions remain. Protect Me, Sic 'Em, Rally Beast, Break Free and Primal Fury cost 150 Action and 100 Mind before Command Efficiency. Recall and doctrine selection have no HAM cost.

Master doctrines

  • Alpha Bond designates one companion. While it is the only called creature and remains alive and in command range, it gains +10 accuracy and 10% direct damage; its handler takes 5% less direct damage.
  • Pack Tactics requires two or three called creatures, each control level 35 or lower. Nearby active companions gain +5 accuracy and a 10% damage bonus divided by the total called creature count.
  • The doctrines are mutually exclusive and require Master Creature Handler. Change them outside combat, with a shared 60-second switch cooldown. Existing total control and pet-count limits are unchanged.

Companion training and roles

  • The Beast Journal now offers six retrainable specialties. Companions share one training point initially, two at 500 bond and three at 2,500 bond.
  • Accuracy grants +5 per rank. Attack Speed shortens attack time by 3% per rank while retaining the normal attack-speed floor. Control Resistance grants +6 state defense per rank.
  • Recovery increases natural HAM regeneration by 10% per rank while both partners are out of combat, and adds two percentage points per rank to Rally recovery.
  • Role Mastery adds two percentage points of Guardian damage reduction, 2% Ravager damage, +3 pursuit accuracy, +50 Venomous poison power or one percentage point of Support owner recovery per rank. Existing recovery caps remain.
  • Command Efficiency reduces technique HAM costs by 10% per rank. Training allocation and reset require both partners out of combat and share a 60-second cooldown.
  • Ravager hits now also apply armor rend for 12 seconds: one shared target stack every five seconds, maximum three. Each stack reduces nonimmune armor resistance by 3% of its value in PvE or 2% in PvP. Immunities and vulnerabilities remain unchanged.
  • Bond labels now progress through Companion, Trusted Companion, Bonded Companion, Veteran Companion and Legendary Companion. Existing bond points and thresholds remain intact.
  • Master Creature Handler gains an additional +20 melee accuracy, +20 ranged accuracy, +25 melee defense, +25 ranged defense, +15 block, +10 counterattack and +20 knockdown defense. These are generalized bonuses, additive to existing profession training.

Companion contracts
