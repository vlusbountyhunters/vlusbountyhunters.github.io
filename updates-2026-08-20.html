Galactic Underworld Update

The Hutts are mobilizing, Black Sun and Death Watch are answering, and lucrative new battles, contracts, and rewards are spreading across VLU Core3.

Faction warfare

The underworld goes to war

  • Defeating members of the Hutt, Jabba, or Borvo factions now awards both Black Sun and Death Watch faction standing.
  • New Hutt Expeditionary Force destroy missions pay 150,000 base credits and pit players against exceptionally tough forces with valuable loot.
  • Large dynamic battles now appear throughout the galaxy, with allied Black Sun and Death Watch forces fighting elite Hutt Expeditionary troops and Hutt-allied battle droids.
  • Black Sun and Death Watch mission targets received attackability corrections.

Earn their loyalty

Underworld allies

  • Reach 5,000 Black Sun standing to gain assistance from nearby Black Sun NPCs
  • Reach 5,000 Death Watch standing to gain assistance from nearby Death Watch NPCs
  • Allied forces respond within 64 meters when creatures or NPCs attack you
  • Black Sun and Death Watch will not turn on one another while helping

Yavin IV contracts

High-risk hunting

  • Primeval Acklay missions are now available on Yavin IV with a 500,000-credit base payout.
  • Geonosian Warrior Strike Team missions pay 350,000 base credits.
  • Geonosian Warriors and their Champions have been substantially strengthened after initial field reports found the encounters too easy.
  • Both mission families feature enhanced encounter-specific loot.

Exploration rewards

Search every container

  • Random loot containers found throughout the world can now contain useful bonus items
  • Bonus finds include weapons, armor and clothing attachments, crystals, and uncommon wearables
  • Location-specific container loot remains intact
  • World containers retain their existing respawn behavior

Loot and equipment

Carry more. Earn more.

01

Junk Dealer windfall

Every Junk Dealer now buys all positively valued loot regardless of dealer specialty and pays 20 times its former junk value. Crafted, altered, no-trade, and filled-container safeguards remain in place.

02

Expanded grenades

Grenades now support dramatically larger stack sizes, and the capacity upgrade applies to existing grenade stacks as well as newly created ones.

03

Useful discoveries

World exploration has a new reward loop: static containers have a chance to add a useful item without replacing their original regional loot.

Galaxy online

All listed changes are live now.
