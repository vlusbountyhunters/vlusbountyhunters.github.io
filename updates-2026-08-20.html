Transmission // 20 August 2026

Galactic Underworld Update

The underworld is at war, the Force is surging, and dangerous new contracts are waiting across the galaxy.

Important before logging in

Run Scan/Update through your launcher.

This update includes new client content required to experience the changes properly.

Launcher guide

Faction warfare

The underworld goes to war

  • Hutt forces will battle both Black Sun and Death Watch.
  • Black Sun and Death Watch forces will turn their weapons against the Hutts.
  • Defeating Black Sun and Death Watch can earn Hutt favor, while fighting the Hutts can improve standing with their enemies.
  • Respected allies may become friendly and assist during nearby battles.
  • Underworld war parties now appear near settlements, outposts, battle sites, and other locations across numerous worlds.
  • Faction behavior has been improved so opposing forces identify and engage one another more reliably.

New contracts

High-risk hunting

  • Elite Hutt Expeditionary Force missions
  • Grueling Acklay hunts on Yavin IV
  • Geonosian warrior strike-team missions
  • Expanded Black Sun, Death Watch, and Hutt contracts
  • More appropriate fortified faction encampments
  • More determined lair and mission defenders

Jedi combat

The Force grows stronger

  • Player Jedi attacks and Force abilities flow more quickly during PvE combat.
  • Force powers strike with greater authority against creatures and hostile NPCs.
  • Force Lightning's lingering flames are now a more serious battlefield threat.
  • Force Lightning Cone abilities erupt across a true area, improving their performance against clustered enemies.
  • These improvements do not accelerate NPC Jedi or alter PvP combat.

Defense and balance

Training comes together

  • Dodge, block, and counterattack can contribute regardless of the equipped weapon
  • Profession training and attachment bonuses form a more complete defensive package
  • The galaxy's most dangerous enemies retain ways to break exceptional defenses
  • Creature defenses remain separate from these player-focused improvements
  • Melee weapon components now help crafted melee arms compete more effectively

Weaponsmith discoveries

Recovered designs return to the forge

01

Carbines

Alliance Needler, Massassi Guardian, and Sfor Republic designs are now available to Master Weaponsmiths.

02

Rifles

DC-15, Proton, Geonosian Drill, LD-1, Massassi Ink, and DP-23 designs have joined the crafting lineup.

03

Pistols

Heroic Capacity Scatter, Intimidator, and Renegade pistol designs are ready for skilled craftsmen.

Spice crafting

Greater power, lasting consequences

  • Crafted spice now provides dramatically stronger positive effects.
  • Spice effectiveness can benefit from experimentation, giving talented crafters more room to distinguish their products.
  • Positive effects offer a much longer-lasting experience while benefiting from normal crafting improvements.
  • Spice has gained greater power, but coming down from it will now linger longer.
  • Thruster Head now enhances Willpower alongside its established benefits.

Groups and Squad Leaders

Together across the galaxy

  • Groups remain effective when members travel to distant worlds
  • Mission rewards are not reduced simply because group members are far apart
  • Squad Leader support reaches group members across the galaxy
  • Organized teams gain greater freedom to operate in different locations

Loot and rewards

Claim the victory you earned

  • Enemies defeated by burning, bleeding, poison, Force effects, or other lingering combat damage now recognize the original attacker.
  • Loot rights, kill credit, experience, pet attribution, and group participation carry through when a lingering effect lands the killing blow.
  • Static world containers can hold additional useful treasures, including weapons, wearables, attachments, and crystals.
  • Junk Dealers now purchase a wider range of valuable loot while retaining important item safeguards.

Quality of life

More room for adventure

  • Grenades can now be stored in convenient stacks
  • Backpacks placed inside player structures can once again be moved or picked up normally
  • Mission, loot, faction, and creature behavior received additional cleanup

Galaxy online
