Transmission // 4 September 2026

The Hunters' Reckoning

The galaxy has crossed a threshold. Contracts once whispered between guild halls now draw teams of hunters into the open, faction strongholds have become true battlefields, and the Outer Rim is alive with enemies, relics, and rewards worth risking everything to claim.

Chapter I // The bounty war

No quarry is beyond reach

Player bounties now stand alongside the galaxy's elite contracts, with rewards set just beneath the legendary VIP hunts. Bounty terminals properly present both player and Jedi missions, and any player capable of equipping a lightsaber can become the subject of a Jedi contract.

Up to three Bounty Hunters may accept the same Jedi mission, form a team, and share completion credit when they remain close to the final fight. The days of one hunter carrying the entire pursuit are over.

Master Bounty Hunter

Hunter's Determination

Masters now have a 75% chance to bypass a player Jedi's lightsaber block with ranged, melee, heavy, special-heavy, and thrown weapons. The ability and its purpose are visible on the profession sheet, and existing Masters receive it automatically.

Chapter II // Control the fight

Strike, recover, pursue

Fire Knockdown and Underhand Shot are dramatically more reliable—especially against player opponents. Underhand Shot now leaves its victim dizzy, while Fire Knockdown adds dizziness and a punishing fire effect. Confusion Shot deals 35% more damage.

Master Bounty Hunters also gain Propelling Boots: a surge that clears removable harmful states, returns the hunter to their feet, and doubles movement speed for 20 seconds. Existing Masters receive the command alongside newly trained hunters.

Defensive doctrine

Built to survive

Carbineer and Bounty Hunter progression now carries substantially stronger melee, ranged, posture, and state defenses. The gains are displayed directly throughout both profession sheets, culminating in 70 melee and 70 ranged defense for fully trained Masters.

Chapter III // The strongholds

Planetary control has teeth

Planetary Headquarters now contribute 50 points toward control and can be fought as real faction objectives. Service terminals, recruiters, mission access, insurance, banking, bazaar service, and destructible objective terminals occupy reachable positions, while corrected barricades and 100-meter battlefield turrets defend the perimeter without sealing the entrance behind invisible obstructions.

Imperial headquarters field AT-ATs, and existing headquarters are reconciled to the renewed layout. Their objective terminals can be reached, sliced, defended, and destroyed as intended.

The Sith Hunters

Black and red at the gates

Sith Hunter Troopers guarding Planetary Headquarters now appear in complete suits of RIS armor with their black-and-red treatment intact. The correction applies when defenders return at already-placed headquarters as well as at newly constructed bases.

Chapter IV // The Outer Rim burns

The invasion is operational

Hardened Black Sun and Death Watch forces now deploy successfully across 17 city, starport, and outpost fronts on Corellia, Tatooine, Rori, Naboo, Dathomir, Endor, Yavin IV, and Lok. Their soldiers reinforce the occupied approaches, and players with at least 5,000 standing in the matching faction are recognized as allies.

Dantooine carries additional underworld activity, rare enemies can emerge dynamically across every enabled ground world, and a Jedi at maximum visibility may draw the personal attention of Boba Fett, Darth Vader, or Mara Jade in one of the galaxy's hardest encounters.

Cries of Alderaan

Rewards both banners can see

Imperial and Rebel participants now receive visible faction rewards from the event: 275 points for the field objective and 550 points at the finale, including the 10% event bonus for the side they serve.

Chapter V // Armor reforged

Protection worthy of the new war

RIS armor is Heavy across the board—new, existing, and crafted—with base effectiveness against stun and lightsaber damage alongside its other protections. Personal Shield Generators are now Medium armor, including existing units, and Bounty Hunter armor is classified as Heavy.

The Nightsister Melee Armguard has returned as a rare prize from Nightsisters and Axkva Min, carrying Medium armor and 80% resistance to every damage type. Swamp Trooper faction rewards can be claimed properly, and Death Watch Overlord blood completion grants a full Mandalorian armor set.

Faction companions

Heavier reinforcements

Every player faction pet except the AT-AT has 75% more HAM, Heavy armor, and stronger resistances. The Nightsister Elder pet now stands much closer to the formidable enemy from which it was inspired.

Chapter VI // Weapons of consequence

Every trigger tells a story

The Beam Rifle is certified at Master Commando, deals lightsaber damage, and carries Heavy armor piercing. Heavy Acid Rifles inflict high-potency poison and fire effects, while every ranged grenade now strikes with Heavy armor piercing. Fast Blast adds intimidation and a powerful mind bleed.

The EE3 Carbine, Tangle Pistol, Geonosian Sonic Blaster, and Berserker Rifle carry Medium armor piercing, with the Berserker dealing kinetic damage. The Elite Carbine is Heavy armor piercing, the Nightsister Energy Lance is Heavy armor piercing, and Nym's Slugthrower Carbine now delivers 7,500–20,000 damage at 0.5 speed with Heavy armor piercing.

Old weapons, new rules

The arsenal remembers

Where these changes affect stored equipment, existing weapons are brought forward with the new damage, armor-piercing, and effect rules rather than being left behind.

Chapter VII // Richer journeys

Every quest can change a fortune

The first completion of every journal quest now awards 25,000–50,000 bank credits and a premium component, with additional chances for a second component and a rare cache or holocron. Galaxywide loot has also grown more varied, bringing rare components, holocrons, and other coveted finds into far more encounters.

Spider Fang, Spider Venom, Donkuwah Poison, and the broader family of Combat Medic components are 50% stronger. Rare drops, stronger medicine, renewed themepark prizes, and expanded quest rewards make exploration worthwhile from the first contract to the deadliest hunt.

Quality of life

More time in the field

Area looting reaches 100 meters, Force Armor and Force Shield last 24 hours, rank-five cities can support 100 trainers, Dathomir destroy missions have more room to find valid placements, and stalled survivors can return to the Old Man to continue their Jedi journey.

The reckoning is live

Choose a contract. Defend a world. Hunt a legend.
