Transmission // 26 August 2026

The Force Awakens in Industry

A mysterious new harvester, coveted components, renewed battlefields, and corrected Jedi encounters have arrived across the galaxy.

Master Architect innovation

Force Resonance Mineral Harvester

Veteran Architects have begun combining advanced engineering with the power of the Force, creating machinery unlike anything found in an ordinary workshop.

  • All Master Architects now learn the new schematic automatically.
  • The black-and-green harvester operates without consuming power or maintenance.
  • Its internal machinery burns with an unmistakable crimson glow.
  • Its exceptional extraction and storage capabilities can be improved further through experimentation.

A worthy undertaking

Rare components required

  • High-Powered Energy Capacitor
  • Quintessence of the Force
  • Specialized high-grade industrial resources
  • Master Architect expertise

These components remain rare prizes worthy of an endgame crafting project.

Perpetual energy source

High-Powered Energy Capacitor

This powerful device supplies the harvester with its perpetual energy source. It can be recovered from forces associated with Nym on Lok, including Nym pirates, guards, patrols, Droidekas, destroyers, and IG assassin droids.

Nym's Bunker is one of the primary hunting grounds for this component, with numerous Nym combatants guarding its depths. The Droid Engineer Cave is another promising destination because of its heavy concentration of Droidekas, assassin droids, and valuable equipment.

Recommended hunt

Travel to Lok

  • Explore Nym's Bunker
  • Clear the Droid Engineer Cave
  • Hunt Nym patrols and elite forces
  • Search dangerous Nym-controlled territory

A self-stabilizing field

Quintessence of the Force

This rare crystal creates the self-stabilizing Force field that allows the harvester to operate without maintenance. It may be discovered by defeating Nightsister Elders, Light Jedi Masters, and Dark Jedi Masters.

The best hunting grounds include the Nightsister Stronghold, Nightsister Elder enclaves, and other dangerous regions of Dathomir. Hunters seeking a deliberate challenge can also pursue Jedi Masters through the mission-terminal system: Imperial terminals lead toward Light Jedi Masters, while Rebel terminals offer opportunities to confront Dark Jedi Masters.

Recommended hunt

Brave Dathomir

  • Challenge the Nightsister Stronghold
  • Search Nightsister Elder enclaves
  • Accept Light Jedi Master contracts from Imperial terminals
  • Accept Dark Jedi Master contracts from Rebel terminals

The Galactic Civil War

Battlefields evolve

  • Selecting the active conflict through Battlefield Status now provides a direct waypoint to the battle.
  • Victorious battlefields return to the galactic war more quickly.
  • Battlefield victory, cleanup, and planetary-control transitions are more reliable.
  • Faction forces, objectives, rewards, and reinforced fortifications now move the war forward with greater stability.

Jedi encounters

Allegiance made clear

  • Light Jedi Masters now carry their proper identity
  • Light Jedi Masters wield weapons appropriate to their allegiance
  • Dark Jedi Masters remain unmistakable opponents
  • Light and Dark Holocrons and the Quintessence of the Force have returned to their intended rarity

Client update required

Update and scan before entering the galaxy.

Fully close the game and launcher, reopen the launcher, and run Update/Scan before logging in. A new client file is required to display and use the Force Resonance Mineral Harvester correctly.
