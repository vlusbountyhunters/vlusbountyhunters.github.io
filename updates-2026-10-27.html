Frontier Expeditions and Faction Requisitions

Close the game and run Scan/Update in the launcher before reconnecting.

Expedition Bureau

  • New expedition offices on Tatooine, Lok, Mandalore, Chandrila and Hoth provide adventure selection, progress, reward claims and expedition purchases.
  • Death Watch: The Stolen Shipment boarding operation takes place aboard a private corvette, with defense relays, an armory battle, a shipment manifest and an escape objective.
  • Black Sun: Obsidian Vault offers stealth, slicing, bribery and assault approaches inside a private mansion, followed by a response-team battle and extraction.
  • The Mandalorian Great Hunt follows a trail through a territorial pack to an ancient beast.
  • Whiteout: The Lost Convoy combines survival supplies, tracking, a warming-station defense, survivor escort and extraction.
  • Contested Salvage is an explicitly opt-in PvP expedition with crash-site tracking, scavengers, cargo defense and technology recovery.
  • Supply Line is a guild campaign for 2–8 participants with camp construction, convoy recovery and perimeter defense.
  • An Unfinished Rivalry adds a recurring nemesis with ten progression tiers.
  • Ruins: Rotating Challenge features an entrance hazard, expedition rescue, a guardian battle and archive recovery.
  • Echoes Beneath the Dust adds surveying, excavation, tomb raiders, fragment assembly and artifact collection.
  • Legacy of the Stolen Shipment unlocks after excavation and either the boarding operation or the vault heist, leading to a sealed Mandalorian ruin and its guardian.
  • Expedition progress, collections and reward claims survive reconnects. Private adventures include return and recovery handling; they are not player storage spaces.

Expedition Rewards

Rewards cost expedition tokens and may require the relevant completed collections. Existing weapon certification requirements remain in effect.

  • Expedition Display Stand: 8 tokens. Vault Expedition Lounge: 8. Boarding Victory Standard: 12. Archaeologist's Archive Display: 18.
  • Breachline Expedition Rifle: 32 tokens; energy damage 14,000–30,000; heavy armor piercing; attack speed 1.5.
  • Obsidian Cipher Pistol: 32 tokens; energy damage 12,000–26,000; heavy armor piercing; attack speed 1.0.
  • Ancient Trailbreaker Vibro-Axe: 40 tokens; kinetic damage 16,000–34,000; heavy armor piercing; attack speed 1.5.
  • Storm of the Lost Archive: 64 tokens; electricity damage 18,000–36,000; heavy armor piercing; attack speed 2.0.
  • All four expedition weapons have 100,000 maximum condition.
  • Exceptional Supply Crate: 32 tokens and the salvage/artifact collections; six loot rolls. Legendary Supply Crate: 80 tokens and the finale/guild/challenge collections; ten loot rolls.
  • Three permanent earned titles: Relic Seeker, Convoy Pathfinder and Keeper of the Lost Archive.

GCW Requisitions

  • Calibrated Ammunition costs 15,000 GCW currency and adds 20% minimum and maximum weapon damage for 1,000 uses, subject to normal powerup compatibility and non-stacking rules.
  • Shield Support Station costs 35,000 GCW currency. It grants eligible nearby faction allies +50 general defense for 60 seconds. This is a defense bonus, not damage absorption or invulnerability.
  • Ammunition Cache costs 20,000 GCW currency and dispenses Calibrated Ammunition.
  • Both new support stations last 20 minutes and share 30 charges. Existing deployment and use cooldowns remain in force.
  • All existing requisition IDs, prices and rewards are preserved.

Black Sun Rewards

  • Ranged and Melee Overchargers cost 25 faction reward tokens each: +30% minimum and maximum weapon damage for 1,000 uses, with normal compatibility and non-stacking restrictions.
  • Shadowweave Operative Chestplate costs 30 tokens: Medium armor, 75% resistance to all nine damage types, 50,000 maximum condition, +30 camouflage, +30 mask scent and +20 ranged defense. It improves existing skills; it does not grant camouflage or mask scent abilities.
  • Emergency Escape Device costs 20 tokens: one use, +50% movement speed for 10 seconds, with a three-minute cooldown. It does not remove combat, faction or bounty status.
  • Exceptional Supplies costs 20 tokens and provides an Exceptional Supply Crate.
  • Existing Black Sun and Death Watch offers remain unchanged.
