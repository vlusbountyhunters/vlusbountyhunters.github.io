Patch notes · November 14, 2026

Faction Recruiter Rewards, Imperial Uniforms and Rank Insignia

Deployed November 14, 2026. Close the game, update or scan through the launcher, then reopen the game before using the new rewards.

Visit your Imperial or Rebel faction recruiter and open the quartermaster catalogue. Imperial clothing is listed under Imperial uniforms and insignia. Faction points and GCW currency are separate balances; the prices below specify which one you need.

The recruiter now opens a categorized quartermaster with item descriptions, separate faction-point and GCW balances, earned unlock requirements and confirmation. Faction purchases preserve at least 200 standing. One outstanding paid claim is saved; a full inventory or unsuitable upgrade target does not charge again. Existing items, paid claims, permanent tiers and standard recruiter services remain available.

Equipment

  • Four complete sets of faction armor: nine Imperial Stormtrooper pieces and six Rebel Marine pieces per role. All Heavy, 100,000 condition. Chests cost 20,000 faction points; other pieces cost 8,000 each.
  • Assault: 80% protection, 100 encumbrance per HAM pool; chest +20 ranged/melee accuracy.
  • Recon: 75%, 40 encumbrance; chest +20 dodge/camouflage and +15 knockdown/dizzy defense.
  • Heavy Support: 85%, 175 encumbrance; chest +25 ranged/+15 melee defense.
  • Officer: 80%, 100 encumbrance; chest +20 injury treatment, +15 treatment speed, +10 ranged defense. All pieces retain normal faction/race restrictions.
  • Five weapons per faction, each 25,000 faction points and Heavy armor piercing: Breach Rifle 12,000–22,000 energy/1.8 speed; Suppression Carbine 9,000–17,000 energy/1.3; Disruption Pistol 8,000–15,000 electricity/1.1; Attrition Rifle 10,000–18,000 acid/1.8; Close Assault Baton 12,000–24,000 stun/1.4.
  • Breach reduces protected body-armor resistance by 5% relative, preserving immunity. Suppression has a 25% chance of -20 melee/ranged accuracy for six seconds; wild level-300+ bosses are immune. Disruption has a 25% chance to remove only the GCW field-shield buff. Attrition has a 25% chance of a 2,000-strength Health poison for twelve seconds, subject to ordinary resistance/cures. These three procs have target-wide twenty-second recharge. Close Assault adds 10% direct non-Force weapon damage within five metres.

Specializations and support

  • Weapon mode switches cost 40,000 GCW. One of the five effects replaces the previous mode, including the built-in Field weapon mode; effects do not stack.
  • Armor modes cost 50,000 GCW per piece: Protection adds two protection points up to 90%; Lightweight reduces positive encumbrance 25% but costs five protection points; Resolve adds +15 ordinary state defense, once across equipped pieces.
  • Walker modes cost 75,000 GCW: Protection adds two protection points, slows walking to 1.7 m/s and adds 0.3 seconds to cannon recharge; Mobility walks at 2.5 m/s but loses five protection points; Rapid Cooling subtracts 0.3 seconds from cannon recharge (1.8-second floor) but loses five protection points. Normal slows apply.
  • Hireling role switches cost 30,000 GCW and apply to an existing owned faction hireling. Assault adds 20% direct damage. Medic/Engineer reduce direct damage 20%. Medic radial treatment restores up to 3,000 owner HAM per pool every fifteen seconds. Engineer radial repair restores up to 20,000 condition to the owner's targeted unmounted walker every thirty seconds, outside combat. Both require eight-metre range and share an owner-wide recharge. No resurrection or repair of disabled walkers. Normal pet command, ownership and combat-target rules remain.
  • Existing permanent GCW tiers remain. All new modes replace rather than accumulate; changing modes costs the same price. Target ownership/type/condition is rechecked.

Repeat purchases and crafting

  • Fifty-use trauma and poison-treatment kits: 2,000 faction points each.
  • Calibrated ammunition: 4,000 faction points; normal +20% damage/1,000-use powerup.
  • Healing/shield support controllers: 10,000/12,000 faction points. Retain existing thirty-charge, twenty-minute, faction/range and shared recharge limits.
  • Weapon/armor repair tools: 3,000 faction points, five uses, quality 75. Normal repair failure, profession/station bonuses and condition-loss rules still apply.
  • Emergency Withdrawal Device: 5,000 faction points, one use; +50% movement and acceleration for ten seconds, shared three-minute recharge. No teleport or removal of combat, bounty or PvP flags.
  • Fifteen existing faction armor schematics, 12,000 faction points each. Master Armorsmith required; ten uses and normal materials/experimentation. These craft the established armor recipes, not guaranteed copies of fixed-stat Field armor.
  • Military power handler/vibro motor: 15,000 faction points, 25 uses; +250 minimum, +500 maximum damage, -0.3 speed, +1,000 condition and zero added HAM costs. Motor also supplies +10 wound chance. Normal compatible crafting slots and recipe contributions apply; final weapon stats depend on the complete crafting process.

Imperial uniforms and wearable insignia

  • The Imperial quartermaster has a new Imperial uniforms and insignia category.
  • Prefect Talmont's Uniform costs 10,000 faction points. It uses his original robe appearance and is restricted to male humanoids because that uniform has only a male fit. Existing NPC outfits are unchanged.
  • Imperial AT-AT Crew and TIE Pilot flight suits cost 6,000 faction points each. They retain their established appearances and normal clothing restrictions.
  • Wearable Imperial rank plaques: Lieutenant (1,000), Captain (2,000), Major (3,000), Commander (4,000), Colonel (5,000), General (6,000), Admiral (7,000), and Moff (8,000 faction points). Each has labelled male/female fits with matching equip restrictions, using the original rank-plaque appearances. Supported humanoids: Bothan, Human, Mon Calamari, Rodian, Sullustan, Trandoshan, Twi'lek and Zabrak.
  • Insignia occupy the necklace slot, one at a time, and may be covered by bulky armor. They do not grant actual military rank, permissions, titles or combat bonuses. All new clothing/insignia require Imperial membership and add no skill modifiers or attachment sockets. Existing officer uniform pieces remain available.
  • This extension adds 19 offers (three uniforms and sixteen insignia fits), taking the new catalogue to 126 offers. Update through the launcher before logging in.

Earned rewards and services

  • Console/table/chest furnishings cost 6,000/8,000/4,000 faction points.
  • Faction medical terminals cost 50,000 GCW and require 5,000 earned campaign points. Their standard doctor/entertainer packages cost 10,000 credits per service; matching faction, six-metre range and out-of-combat rules apply. Existing stronger buffs are protected and fresh duplicate purchases rejected.
  • Named dress jackets require 5,000 career points and 25,000 GCW; campaign standards require 10,000 points and 30,000 GCW. Established appearances; no combat modifiers.
  • Distinguished Imperial/Rebel citations require 15,000 career points, a victorious finale and 10,000 GCW. Recipient achievements are rechecked. The cosmetic title remains selectable after resignation; failed unlocks leave the citation intact.
  • Reusable equipment finishing kit costs 25,000 GCW and requires 5,000 career points to purchase/use. Supports existing armor/walker palette channels only; fixed-color models are explicitly excluded. No statistics or condition changes.

Existing equipment and earned progression are retained. These notes describe the deployed update; in-game appearance, layering and fit still need player feedback. If you encounter an issue, report the item name, your character's species and the action you attempted.
