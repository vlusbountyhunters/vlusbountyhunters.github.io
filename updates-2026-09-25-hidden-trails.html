Field transmission // 25 September 2026

Hidden Trails, Familiar Companions

The Ranger crossed the clearing, paused beneath the branches, and vanished. Back at the outpost, a handler waited beside a Blackwing Rancor's growth chamber. Somewhere beyond the perimeter, the Annihilator's familiar burst answered the morning.

The clearing falls silent

Ranger camouflage now hides the concealed player and their equipment from other players, rather than leaving the character visibly standing in the open. Players arriving later receive the same concealment. When the buff ends or is removed, the character becomes visible again.

You retain your own local avatar for movement and control. Camouflage duration, kit consumption and detection rules have not changed. Mask Scent is unchanged; this is not invulnerability or a guarantee against creature detection.

A bond is counted fairly

The handler's faction companion stepped aside. It no longer occupied a place in the Creature Handler's creature-control allowance merely because it looked like an animal.

Crafted pet activation and creature-pet summoning now count only Creature Handler pets toward that allowance. This repairs the false control-level rejection that faction creatures could cause when claiming companions such as a Bio-Engineer Blackwing Rancor. The correction applies to existing deeds and companions as well as new ones.

Pet combat stats are preserved. Real creature-level limits, active-pet limits, aggressive-pet skill requirements and normal activation restrictions still apply. If your actual Creature Handler pets fill the allowance, store one before calling another.

The Annihilator remembers its stance

The gunner settled the Annihilator into its intended low-held stance. A later companion update had displaced that stance; the missing animation state has now been restored without replacing the newer pet animation states.

The weapon keeps its model, sound, Heavy armor piercing, combat stats and six-shot timed burst. Close the game and run Launcher Scan/Update before testing the repair. Please report any remaining wrong stance, especially while moving, kneeling or prone.

A clear route back to the galaxy

The recent launcher repair addresses the file-in-use error that could prevent a downloaded launcher update from being installed. Launcher version 2026.9.25.2 retains the current download service and patch-note feed.

If an older launcher still fails while installing its own update, close all launcher windows and use the Vlu launcher repair updater, then run Scan/Update before reconnecting.

The oaths and the hunt endure

The recent Death Watch and Black Sun memberships, four-faction character sheet, Death Watch bunker access and armor-component shop, pet-name and activation repairs remain part of the galaxy. Their story continues in Oaths Beyond the War.

Player-funded bounties, revenge offers, Propelling Boots, Bounty Hunter profession descriptions, Nal Hutta discovery badges and restored item names are covered in A Price on Every Shadow. Separate Death Watch and Black Sun emblems above member players are still unfinished and are not included in this release.
