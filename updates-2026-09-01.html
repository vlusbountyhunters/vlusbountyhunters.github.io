Transmission // 1 September 2026

Jedi Mastery Reforged

The final teachings of the pre-Publish 9 Jedi profession have been renewed. Light and Dark Jedi now advance through stronger Master branches, deadlier Force powers, more capable healing, and a lightsaber path worthy of the title of Jedi Master.

Light and Dark mastery

The final path grows stronger

Both Light and Dark Jedi Master trees have received equivalent improvements. Passive teachings throughout the profession have been strengthened, advanced Force abilities have become more formidable in PvE, and the familiar pre-9 identity of every branch remains intact.

Completing the final Jedi Master box now strengthens every damage-dealing lightsaber ability learned throughout Padawan, Journeyman, and Master progression.

Master Novice

Prepared for the trials ahead

  • Force Run II now belongs to Master Novice
  • Force Resist States now belongs to Master Novice
  • Force Knockdown III has moved to Master Force Manipulation I
  • Force Speed II remains at Journeyman Force Power III
  • Force Weaken I remains at Journeyman Master

Lightsaber mastery

Every form reaches its culmination

Master Saber progression has been strengthened across every branch. One-Hand Flurry II, Two-Hand Phantom, and Double-Bladed Dervish II now stand as signature high-level attacks with improved reach, power, speed, and combat effects.

Master Saber IV

Generation Four

  • One-handed Generation Four certification and schematic
  • Two-handed Generation Four certification and schematic
  • Double-bladed Generation Four certification and schematic
  • All three styles are awarded together at Master Saber IV

Master Healing

Restoration worthy of a Jedi Master

Master healing abilities have been substantially strengthened for the galaxy's expanded combat pools. Health, Action, and Mind healing now provide meaningful restoration to allies, while Total Heal Self and Total Heal Other offer comprehensive recovery in PvE.

Total Heal Other will now remain available after logging out and returning to the galaxy.

Force Power

Protection, control, and endurance

  • Force Power I — Force Absorb I
  • Force Power II — Force Intimidate II
  • Force Power III — Drain Force
  • Force Power IV — Force Absorb II

Force Manipulation

Command the battlefield

  • Manipulation I — Force Knockdown III
  • Manipulation II — Regain Consciousness
  • Manipulation III — Force Lightning Cone II
  • Manipulation IV — Force Weaken II

Master Force powers

The Force answers with greater fury

  • Force Intimidate II has become a powerful Master-level PvE suppression ability
  • Force Lightning Cone II is considerably more deadly against PvE enemies
  • Force Weaken I and II remain meaningful against the galaxy's strongest opponents
  • Stronger versions of Intimidate and Weaken now properly replace their weaker forms

Force Sensitive progression

Greater challenges bring greater growth

Force Sensitive combat experience now responds meaningfully to the strength of the enemy defeated. Trivial opponents offer little progress, while challenging and exceptionally dangerous enemies provide substantially greater advancement.

The pre-9 legacy

Renewed, not replaced

The recognizable original Jedi profession remains at the heart of this overhaul. The classic Master branches and abilities endure, but every step toward mastery now carries the strength and purpose expected of the galaxy's most powerful warriors.

Client update required

Fully close and reopen the launcher before entering the galaxy.

The launcher will deliver the renewed Jedi Master skill tables. Return to the galaxy, follow the path of Light or Dark, and discover what it truly means to become a Jedi Master.
