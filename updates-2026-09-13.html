Vlu Update // 13 September 2026

The Weight of Knighthood

Mastery is not the end of a Jedi’s journey.

It is the moment training gives way to responsibility—when the question is no longer whether you can wield the Force, but what you are prepared to face because of it.

A new path to Knighthood has opened on Vlu. Personal trials and new PvE raid encounters await those ready to take that next step. Their challenges are yours to discover.

But remember: a Knight’s title is not a shield.

It is a declaration. And the galaxy will answer.

A Title Earned

Full Jedi Master training in your chosen alignment is now required to receive the Knight invitation. Mastery opens the door; the Knight Trials determine what comes next.

To begin this new chapter together, existing Knight status and all Force Ranking System ranks, experience, and progression are receiving a one-time reset.

Former Knights will receive a popup upon login directing them to meditate at a Force shrine. The Master requirement still applies.

This reset is limited to Knight and FRS progression. Your other professions, possessions, and unrelated quest progress are not being reset.

Your Rank, Earned and Kept

After the one-time reset, ordinary FRS experience losses can reduce your experience, but will not demote your earned rank.

Defeat still carries a cost. It does not erase the rank you have earned.

Power Has Consequences

The new PvE encounters expand the Jedi journey. They do not remove its dangers.

Upon earning Knighthood, you become fully overt and attackable by any player—including members of your own faction.

Jedi also remain on bounty hunter terminals. The hunters are still watching, and the arrival of new trials does not end the hunt.

Some challenges you will choose to face.

Others will choose you.

Number Formatting

Thousands separators have been added to supported credit and numeric displays:

1234567 → 1,234,567

This is a display-only improvement. Balances, costs, and underlying values are unchanged.

Launcher Update

The latest launcher upgrade fixes the issue preventing an already-updated client from opening a second game instance.

Download the Vlu launcher upgrade — R4

Close the launcher before installing. Keep your existing launcher installed and select its actual installation folder—not the game folder. Close all game instances before scanning or applying game updates.
