Transmission // 18–19 August 2026

Combat Freedom & Crafting Update

Expanded combat choices, stronger components, faster production, improved creature behavior, and new quality-of-life features are now live on VLU Core3.

Combat and professions

More freedom. Fewer limits.

  • The combined +25 stat limit and additional +125 ceiling on armor and clothing attachment bonuses have been removed. Individual attachments remain limited to +25.
  • Pistol, carbine, and rifle abilities can now be used across all three conventional ranged weapon types.
  • Profession ownership remains enforced: profession-specific abilities still require the appropriate profession skills.
  • Properly certified launcher pistols can use pistol, carbine, and rifle abilities.
  • Light lightning cannons and other special-heavy weapons now support ability ranges up to 64 meters.
  • Level 200 and higher enemies now produce maximum-strength +25 armor or clothing attachments whenever an attachment drops. Drop rates and unrelated loot are unchanged.
  • Exceptionally high player defenses now receive their full contribution in combat calculations.

Creatures and lairs

Smarter encounters

  • Lair defenders now move toward and protect their lair instead of running away from it
  • General creature aggro range has been reduced while remaining slightly higher than the vanilla range
  • Creature and defense calculations received additional consistency fixes

Crafting and components

Stronger materials

  • Armor components now generate in the same large-stack range as weapon and chemical components.
  • Armor-component effectiveness and resistance values have increased by 30%, including existing components.
  • Large armor, weapon, and chemical component stacks now have a right-click Split Stack option that preserves their crafted values.
  • Lightsaber crystals and pearls remain individual items and are excluded from component stacking.
  • Factories now finish production instantly, regardless of how many items are being manufactured.
  • Missing Master Tailor shirt schematics have been restored.

Looting

Clear the battlefield

  • Area looting is available with /loot area
  • Area-loot range is now 32 meters
  • Normal targeted corpse-looting distance is unchanged
  • Individual ownership and group-loot rules remain enforced

Vehicles and stability

Safe migrations

Component-stack and effectiveness upgrades now persist correctly and will not be repeatedly applied.

Component splitting, factory output, inventory capacity, and component ownership receive additional safeguards.
