Transmission // 31 August 2026

The Jedi Path Reforged

The ancient teachings have been restored and strengthened. Padawans and Journeymen now grow more naturally into the formidable Jedi remembered from the pre-Publish 9 era, while existing Jedi inherit the renewed teachings.

Padawan foundations

The first steps carry greater purpose

The familiar Padawan branches remain intact, but most passive teachings have been strengthened, Force reserves improved, and Force abilities made less exhausting.

  • Animal Scare, Animal Calm, Animal Attack, and Jedi Mind Trick now belong to Padawan Novice.
  • Force Power I now teaches Force Speed I.
  • Force Power II now teaches Resist Disease.
  • Force Power III is a deliberate passive advancement.
  • Force Power IV now teaches Resist Poison.

Padawan saber progression

A weapon worth carrying

  • Your first training lightsaber remains useful throughout early Padawan training
  • Gen-1 construction and certification arrive at Padawan Saber IV
  • Gen-2 construction and certification arrive upon mastering Padawan
  • The first training saber is now a powerful and dependable Jedi weapon without draining its wielder's Force

Power earned, not worn

The strength belongs to the Jedi

The Force Pool and regeneration once tied to the Padawan robe now belong permanently to the Padawan. The robe remains a symbol of achievement, but changing clothing no longer removes power the Jedi has already earned.

Padawan Masters also receive substantially stronger resistance to knockdowns and forced posture changes.

Force Manipulation

The traditional path remains

  • Force Run I
  • Force Knockdown I
  • Mind Blast I
  • Force Throw I

Light and Dark Journeyman

The Force answers with greater fury

Both Light and Dark Journeyman paths have received the same renewed teachings. Most passive abilities have been strengthened, healing has become more effective, and offensive Force powers are considerably more deadly in PvE.

Mind Blast II remains part of Journeyman Novice training, but now carries the power expected from an experienced Jedi.

Journeyman Saber IV

A defining milestone

  • Sweep II
  • One-Hand Flurry II
  • Gen-3 lightsaber construction and certification
  • No additional saber generation is granted at Journeyman Master

Force Power

Protection, speed, and sacrifice

  • Force Power I — Force Armor I
  • Force Power II — Force Shield I
  • Force Power III — Force Speed II
  • Force Power IV — Transfer Force

Force Manipulation

Command the battlefield

  • Manipulation I — Force Lightning Single I
  • Manipulation II — Force Knockdown II and Force Intimidate I
  • Manipulation III — Force Throw II and Force Choke
  • Manipulation IV — Force Lightning Single II

Journeyman combat

Control the space around you

One-Hand Flurry II has become a devastating crowd attack, striking enemies around the Jedi while inflicting Blind, Dizzy, Stun, and Intimidate. Force Intimidate I now reaches hostile opponents surrounding the Jedi while continuing to respect normal combat and state defenses.

Journeyman Master

Teachings of an accomplished Jedi

  • Force Armor II
  • Force Shield II
  • Force Lightning Cone I
  • Total Heal Self
  • Force Weaken I
  • Improved Jedi and lightsaber toughness
  • Stronger knockdown and posture-change defenses

Client and server update

Fully close and reopen the launcher before entering the galaxy.

The launcher will deliver the updated Jedi skill tables. These changes preserve the recognizable pre-9 profession while making every step from newly awakened Padawan to accomplished Journeyman feel powerful, meaningful, and worthy of the Force.
